## verify
make verify: ruff clean, mypy gate/api/site no issues, 2662 passed, 75 skipped, 89 deselected (exit 0).

Deviations from the plan, and why:
- Only the host lookups go on the pool; the rest of each reconciliation (a racer's conversion,
  closing the record) runs in order on the request thread, because the DynamoDB store is a boto3
  resource (not shared across threads anywhere else) and the committed-file cache is a plain dict.
  The per-pull-request lock (an RLock on Context) is held by live_state and by the whole of
  reconcile, so concurrent readers never look a pull request up twice at once or convert it twice.
- The plan's "one httpx.Client per get_pull_request instead of four" was already true of the code;
  the seam test pins it. What the pool did expose was the installation-token cache: eight threads
  on a cold process minted eight tokens; it is minted under a lock now (red above).
- frontier.stale_all_but iterates a copy of ctx.files, since a reconcile under a lock in another
  thread may add an entry while it runs.
