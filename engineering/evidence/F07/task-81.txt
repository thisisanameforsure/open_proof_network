F07-T81 — a target's words and reports do not make its building pull requests stale
(2026-11-19 run, finding B1; the merge actor, graph .github/workflows/merge.yml, pick.py)

THE FINDING
  Six of nine agents on 2026-11-19 reported it: within one target's lane every merge put every
  queued building pull request (proof, partial, alternate, proposal, witness) behind, so it was
  updated and re-gated (3-5 min each). #1552 went green at least six times, #1557 three times.
  Part of the cause: `gates_on` counted every path in the PR's own target, so a postmortem, an
  explainer, a gloss or a literature record merged ahead of a proof made the proof stale, though
  no check a proof runs opens any of those files. (The other part, appends queued behind building
  pull requests, is F07-T82.)

THE FIX (graph branch t1119-actor, .github/workflows/merge.yml)
  - `inert_role(path)`: the actor's own small grammar for the eight accepted roles (pick.py cannot
    import the gate), returning the gate's role name or None.

    Lane None (a pull request in no single target) is unchanged: everything but products counts.
  - The narrating comment at the top of merge.yml is updated in F07-T82's commit with the rest.
