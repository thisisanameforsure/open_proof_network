F07-T60 — re-render the products without a merge (audit 2026-10-04)
====================================================================

Graph branch: audit-b-workflows, commit dd60a8b49 (new .github/workflows/render.yml; gate.yml's
postmerge find step and postmerge_batch.py). Not pushed.

Finding. The post-merge job renders the products only for a merged pull request; a re-pin is the
owner's direct push of gate-spec.json, so it ended "did not merge a pull request; nothing to record"
and the products kept the old gate's statuses until the next merge (Log 2026-09-19: the re-render was
a second, hand-made curator commit).

Fix.
- render.yml, on workflow_dispatch only: checks out main, requires every targets/*/gate-spec.json to
  agree on one pin (else fails naming them), extracts postmerge_batch.py from main's own gate.yml (one
  copy of the loop), checks the pin out as network-render, runs its `products` command (present on
  the current pin 3422bb4: inert-safe), commits `gate: render <pin12>` and pushes through `publish`
  with RENDER=1. permissions: {} at top, contents: write on the job; OPN_GRAPH_DEPLOY_KEY in the push
  step only; OPN_SITE_DEPLOY_TOKEN in the site step only (the render commit is the bot's, so the
  post-merge job its push starts skips the site dispatch — this step stands in for it).
- postmerge_batch.py: publish(render=True) does not stop at "nothing owed" — a refused push renders
  again on main as it is; a re-pin under it ends it (that push dispatches its own render). rebuild()
  takes env SUBJECT for the render's commit subject.
- gate.yml postmerge find step: on a push that merged no pull request, not authored by opn-gate, whose
  range (github.event.before..sha, else sha^..sha) touched targets/*/gate-spec.json, dispatches
  render.yml (the job already has actions: write). A failed dispatch is a warning.
