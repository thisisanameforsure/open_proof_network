Fix
---
* api/opn_api/config.py: `open_appends_per_identity`, env `OPN_API_OPEN_APPENDS_PER_IDENTITY`,
  default `DEFAULT_OPEN_PRS_PER_IDENTITY` (10); published in `rate_limit_policy` (info.json) as
  `open_appends_per_identity`, beside the two existing caps. The global cap is unchanged.
* api/opn_api/submissions.py: `check_open_caps(ctx, identity, branch)` (called from `open_pr`,
  which knows the branch). The new pull request's half is `pending.branch_sub_lane(branch)`; the
  caller's open records are counted in that half only, classified by the one helper the queue uses
  (`pending.sub_lane`: the cached open listing's head_ref, else the record's kind; no host read
  of its own). Appends against `open_appends_per_identity`, building pull requests against the
  existing `open_prs_per_identity`. The refusal keeps the code `open-pull-requests-cap` and names
  which cap: the message says "appends open ... the cap for appends is N
  (open_appends_per_identity)" or "building pull requests open ... (open_pull_requests_per_identity)",
  and `details` carries `cap_name` and `half` beside `cap` and `open`.
* gate/agents/AGENTS.md: the caps paragraph describes the two halves and the three published caps.

Rework: the cap split follows the actor's words sub-lane (lead, after F07-T81/T82)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The split is now words and reports vs everything else, by the same helper as the queue
(`pending.is_words_kind` / `pending.sub_lane`, see task-83.txt's rework): postmortems, glosses,
explainers, their signatures (approval), literature and its confirmation, approach records count
against `open_appends_per_identity`; everything else, annexes and the other `append/` records
included, against `open_pull_requests_per_identity`. The env var keeps the committed name
OPN_API_OPEN_APPENDS_PER_IDENTITY and the published key `open_appends_per_identity` (the lead's
suggested OPN_API_OPEN_WORDS_PER_IDENTITY arrived after the commit; renaming is the lead's call).

`open_pr` takes `words: bool = False` (it knows the branch, not the kind); `appends.append_pr`
passes `words = branch_prefix == append/ and is_words_kind(kind, node_id)`; every other caller
(proposals, submissions, reviews) opens building pull requests and passes nothing.
`check_open_caps(ctx, identity, *, words=False)`. The refusal's `details.half` is "words" or
"build"; the message says "words and reports open (...)" or "pull requests open other than words
and reports (...)", and names the cap (`details.cap_name`).
