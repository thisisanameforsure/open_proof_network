F07-T19 (R20, AC41): the round trip is refused before the merge.
2026-09-17, the same day as T18, after the calibration run's corrections.

WHY, IN ONE LINE
  T18 put the round-trip refusal in the post-merge job, which runs after a pull request has
  merged: a partial with a bad hole would merge, the job would fail, and the graph would be left
  with no products and no bot commit, the failure that took every product down that morning. The
  refusal belongs where the extractor's report already exists pre-merge: step 4, in every caller.

WHAT CHANGED
  gate/opn_gate/steps/artifact.py   `_partial_problems` gains `hole-not-roundtrip` over every hole
                                    whose `closed_roundtrip` is false, beside the offload rule;
                                    details name the holes (`holes`) and carry name, type and
                                    printed closed type (`unwritable`). `run()` already stores the
                                    artifact record before returning the failure, so the verdict's
                                    reader sees every hole with its flag.
  gate/opn_gate/postmerge.py        comment only: step 4 refuses first; the loop is the backstop
                                    for a partial merged under a pin that predates the check.
  gate/tests/golden/artifact-types.json   regenerated: the lean-tier golden test had been red since
                                    T18 because `Hole.as_dict()` gained a field the golden lacked.
