F07-T59 — a standalone sweep for merges with no gate credit (audit 2026-10-04)

Proposed ledger line: T59 ✓ gate/tools/uncredited.py + .github/workflows/credit-sweep.yml (daily, read-only); live sweep: 4 owed, all four acknowledged pending the owner.

Proposed Q entry (F07-Q-next): The post-merge job is the only writer of a merge's gate credit, and a run that
fails before its push leaves the merge uncredited with nothing red on the graph. A daily sweep in the network
repo (not the graph, D-35; watch-upstream precedent F12-Q1) walks main's first-parent line and fails when a
merge that touched a target has no gate: commit naming it (replays included). The subject grammars are copies
of the graph helper's MERGE_RE and CREDIT_RE, held equal by a test against the graph's origin/main gate.yml.
Merges that touch no target are maintenance and owe nothing (the workflow writes no record for them).
The four merges the audit found (#2, #8, #72, #73) are in ACKNOWLEDGED with the reason "pending the owner's
decision: acknowledge or replay" so the sweep is green on everything else; the owner's call is, per merge,
to acknowledge it for good (rewrite the reason) or replay it (the entry then reports as stale and is removed).
