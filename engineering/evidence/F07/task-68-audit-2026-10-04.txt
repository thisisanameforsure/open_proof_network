== The fix ==
  - mcp/reads.listing: cached per path at the head of main (frontier.pin_head; when the head is
    unknown, for frontier_max_stale_s and aged by the same freshness generation as the files).
    Held: the last listing of the path is served; with none, a ToolError
    {error: "host-budget-exhausted", message: <hostbudget.read_message>, source: "graph"}.
  - precheck.advance(read=True) from GET /precheck/<id> only: at most one host read per job per
    precheck_poll_min_s (default 10, OPN_API_PRECHECK_POLL_MIN_S), none while held; the record
    is answered as it stands, and the route adds Retry-After (pending.retry_after) when the budget
    is why — the shape the held submission reads already use. POST /submissions and POST /tokens
    still advance unthrottled: their answer depends on the job's latest state.
  - pending.gate_verdict: held -> null verdict, not cached; a failed read is remembered per head
    sha for verdict_retry_s (default 60, OPN_API_VERDICT_RETRY_S).
  - pending._annex_unrendered: kept per (merge sha, rendered sha, directory); held -> treated as
    a host that cannot say (waiting_on_products leaves the pull request unchanged, C7).
  - Tables are bounded (pending.MAX_REMEMBERED = 4096; forget_old drops entries past their
    window first).
