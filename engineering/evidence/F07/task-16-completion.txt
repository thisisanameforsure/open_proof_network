2. The change
-------------
api/opn_api/submissions.py  imports pending; after open_pr answers and the log line:
    pending.record(ctx, identity, submission_id=submission_id, kind=artifact_type,
                   target_id=claim.target_id, node_id=node_id, pr=pr, precheck_job_id=job.id)
  The names were checked against the code: pending.record's keyword arguments are exactly these;
  kind is the artifact type, which is what pending.ATTESTING_KINDS is held equal to. No import
  cycle: pending imports app, clock, frontier, githost and store, never submissions. The response
  body is unchanged; a store failure inside record is logged and the route still answers 201
  (pending.record's own contract, test_pending_submissions.py covers it for the appends).
