reaches a hole's statement is a binder the assembly introduced by destructuring a proved fact (hp,
hq here; Qc/hQc/Qx/hQx/Aq/hAq on the live erdos-1050--h1-v2--h3 and --h4). Those are what the
extractor now marks. The live erdos-1050--h1-v2--h2 (from graph PR #196, four proved `have`s
before its hole) confirms it: its statement has no hypotheses and its slot is
`theorem witness : True`. The "about 250 lines" in the amendment note was the tester's prediction
of that witness in the MCP log, not what the gate asked.

Marked proved: the field binders of `I.casesOn … major (fun fields => …)` for a structure-like I

branch can be reached). Narrowed type: each run of unproved binders is `∃ vars, hyps ∧ rest` as
before; each run of proved binders is ∀-bound in front of `rest` (and dropped if nothing follows),
so data the assembly obtained is given to the witness, not chosen by it. A witness of the full type
is also accepted, so no hole is harder to witness than before the rule.
