Where the rule overrides an earlier decision (Q29 call 4)
  F07-T7 (Mike, 2026-09-13) made a hole that restates an existing sibling a dependency edge to that node.
  Under v3.19 an edge the parent would wait on is what a route must not add, so the edge is gone for a
  sibling that is not one of the parent's own holes; the reuse stays in the job's holes block. The two
  F07-T7 tests were rewritten to the new rule and say so in their docstrings. A finisher who needs that
  sibling's theorem inlines it; the DAG loses that one edge.

What this does and does not do to the live graph
  The gate binds a graph only at the re-pin (2026-09-17). Until the graph's gate-spec.json pins a commit
  carrying this change, its products are rendered by the old reading: the seven live parents stay blocked
  and the service keeps answering 409 for them. After the re-pin, the next render derives each of them
  ready with no record rewritten, and a second route on any of them is accepted. Nothing needs a migration.
