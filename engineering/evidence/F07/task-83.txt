Finding
-------
B1 (6 of 9 agents): green appends sat 20-50 min behind proofs and partials on their target. The
merge actor now splits each target's lane into two halves that do not wait for each other
(F07-T81/T82, another agent, the graph's merge.yml): appends (append/ branches) and building pull

for every lane. The service's `queue` block (GET /submissions/<id>) and each entry's `queue` in
GET /submissions.json counted the whole target lane, so an append was told it stood behind
proofs that can no longer delay it.

Fix (api/opn_api/pending.py)
----------------------------
* `Lane = (target_id, "append" | "build")`; `UNPLACED = ("*", "*")`.
* `sub_lane(record, entry)`: the open listing's `head_ref` decides (the actor decides by the
  branch: build on propose/ or submit/, append otherwise). Fallback, with no listing entry: the
  record's `kind` (`BUILD_KINDS` = POST /submissions' artifact types + speculative, variant,
  witness). Justification: the record's kind decided the branch when the route opened it —
  POST /submissions opens on submit/ (racers' conversion keeps submit/), proposals.open_proposal
  (speculative, variant, witness) on propose/, every other route through appends.append_pr or
  reviews on append/ (or review/, curate/, which the actor does not take). With neither, build
  (the stricter half). `branch_sub_lane(branch)` is the same rule for a branch about to be opened
  (used by F07-T84's caps).
* `place(ctx, listed, records, order)` gives every pull request of the actor's order its Lane;
  `lane_queue` keeps the same (target, half) plus every UNPLACED one; an UNPLACED pull request
  itself still counts the whole order. `queue_block`, `entry_queue` and `snapshot` all go through
  `place`, so the per-id answer and the listing agree.
* QUEUE_ORDER and QUEUE_NOTE describe the two halves; the guide's queue passage
  (gate/agents/AGENTS.md, "One gate round is not the time to merge") says so and drops "an annex
  or a postmortem ... can wait one round behind a proof", which is no longer true.

Rework: the actor's rule is narrower (lead, after F07-T81/T82 landed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The actor's words sub-lane holds only `append/` pull requests whose every path has an inert role
(merge.yml `inert_role`: postmortem, precheck-record, explainer, explainer-signature, gloss,
gloss-signature, approach-record, literature). Annexes, shelvings, review records, steward and
panel records, display names, defect claims and revision requests are on `append/` but stay in
the target's building sub-lane.

Service side, keyed on the record's kind (cheaper than paths), one named constant:
`pending.WORDS_KINDS_ON_NODE` = postmortem, gloss, explainer, approval (a steward's signature of a
gloss or explainer: approvals.signature_path writes under gloss/ or explainer/), literature,
literature-confirmation (literature.record_path: literature/<ts>-<login>.yaml); and
`WORDS_KINDS_ON_TARGET` = approach-record (approaches/<name>.yaml). Kinds recorded by the routes
(grep of `append_pr(`/`kind=` across api/opn_api) and their mapping:
  words:    postmortem, gloss, explainer, approval, literature, literature-confirmation (on a node);
            approach-record (on the target)
  building: annex, shelving, steward, panel kinds, display-name, registration, revision-request,
            defect-claim, gloss-withdrawal (writes withdrawals/, not inert), review, candidate,
            review-answer, review-signature, pair-proof, proof/partial/... (submit/),
            speculative/variant/witness (propose/), and any kind not named.
A gloss or explainer with no node (a target's or a definition module's) is building: inert_role
only takes paths under targets/<t>/nodes/<n>/ (or approaches/). The precheck-record role has no
service record kind (no route records one). `sub_lane(record, entry)`: words only if the kind is
words AND, where the listing carries the pull request, its head_ref is append/; else building.
No record: building (it is UNPLACED anyway). `branch_sub_lane`, `BUILD_KINDS`, `BUILD_PREFIXES`
and `SUB_APPEND` are gone; `SUB_WORDS = "words"`.
