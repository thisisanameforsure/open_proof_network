Reading. A clean screen costs seconds, not minutes, on both a core-only and a Mathlib statement: the
four tactics fail fast. The cost is in a *finding* — replaying the found proof through leanchecker
--fresh is ~55 s even for a one-line tautology, because --fresh re-checks every import. So the
provisional budgets in §6 (60 s per attempt, 5 min per subject) are generous for the attempts and
tight for a subject with several findings: a subject where three screens all find something would
spend ~3 min in replays alone. Keep 60 s per attempt; the per-subject budget should be read as
"attempts plus replays", which is what the runner counts. The 0.0 s consequence rows are vacuous
passes (no qa/consequences/<Name>.lean); a real consequence screen costs one attempt each.
