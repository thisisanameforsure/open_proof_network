$$\sum_{a\in S} \frac{1}{2^a-1} \ne \frac12 \quad\text{for every finite } S \subseteq \mathbb{N}.$$

A finite sum of reciprocals of odd numbers is a rational with odd denominator (the term at $a = 0$ is Lean's $0$ and changes nothing), and one half is not. So any representation of one half by this series is infinite, which is what makes the greedy walk the only candidate.
