## Verdict
Mathlib has Chebyshev-strength bounds only: θ(x) ≥ (log 2 − o(1))x (via psi_ge + psi_sub_theta_le)
and θ(x) ≤ (log 4)x. Their ratio is 2, so they give primes in (x, (2+ε)x] style windows,
never (x, 1.1x]: θ(1.1x) − θ(x) ≥ 0.693·1.1x − 1.386x < 0. No PNT and no short-window
(x, (1+ε)x] lemma found. h_cheb's window (k/2, 11k/20] therefore needs PNT (or an explicit
Rosser–Schoenfeld/Dusart-type bound) as a hole — consistent with a03's partial whose hole is PNT.
