F22-T29 — an explainer of a merged partial is named the way list_words_needed names it; words
with step anchors are never filed as a gloss (2026-11-19 run, finding B10; F14's first half)

Finding
-------
list_words_needed's row of a merged partial says kind `partial`, with the artifact's hash as
`lean_hash`. Agents sent that back:
- {kind: partial, node_id, proof: <hash>} → 400 subject-invalid "a partial's subject names the
  merged partial by its path under the node" (the partial-gloss rule of F29-T9 caught it, since
  `partial` was a gloss kind and `proof` was ignored); the path in `proof` failed the same way.
- {kind: partial, node_id, module: <path>} with an explainer's anchored text → 201, filed as a
  *gloss* of the partial (route approach words), section `whole`, anchors printed as text.
- kind `alternate` (also a row kind) → "subject.kind is one of statement, ..., proof".
Only {kind: proof, node_id, proof: <hash>} worked, and nothing said so.

Fix
---
api/opn_api/glosses.py
- `subject_of`: a subject of an artifact kind (proof, alternate, partial — the row kinds,
  `glosses.ARTIFACT_KINDS`) that names `proof` is an explainer; it comes back with kind `proof`, so
  everything downstream is the explainer path it always was. `alternate` without `proof`, `proof`
  on a non-artifact kind, `module` and `proof` together, and a `proof` that is not a SHA-256 are
  each `subject-invalid` with one sentence naming the accepted shape (EXPLAINER_SHAPE: kind as the
  row gives it, proof the row's lean_hash or attempts.assemblies[].hash, never a path). The
  partial-gloss refusal in `where` names both shapes.
- `check_gloss_names_no_steps` (after the tree is materialised): words with a level-2 heading
  ending in `{steps: ...}` sent with any gloss subject are refused `400 subject-invalid`, not
  filed. For a partial named by `module`, `details.explainer_subject` is the exact subject to send,
  `{kind: partial, node_id, proof: <hash of that file>}`. (The gate already warned on such a gloss
  after merge, `gloss-step-anchors`, F-D7; the service now stops it before anything opens.)
gate/opn_gate/codes.py: the `subject-invalid` catalog row says the new kinds, the anchor rule and
the remedy.
api/opn_api/mcp/writes.py: submit_gloss's subject.kind enum gains `alternate`; its description
says an explainer's kind and proof are the row's. Kept within the tools/list budget (the first
wording broke test_the_whole_tools_list_fits_its_budget by 31 characters; shortened).
gate/agents/AGENTS.md ("Through the service" and the explainer paragraph): the explainer subject
takes the row's kind; `{kind: partial, module}` is the route's words; a JSON example of a
partial's explainer beside the existing explainer example (not an executed block, since the
fixture has no merged partial). The paragraph and output block after it were also restated for
F22-T28 (the fixture's anchored explainer now answers products-pending).

list_words_needed was not given a per-row subject object: the brief preferred making the
advertised kind work, which this does, and the HTTP twin of that tool is two committed product
files, which could not carry one anyway.
