Finding 2 (2026-10-08 tester A, erdos-1094): `div-zero` flags `n % p` with `Nat.Prime p` in scope.
Verdict: reproduced, and by design. No code changed.

Spec: F02-R2 defines div-zero as "division or modulo whose divisor is not syntactically non-zero";
gate/lean/OpnGate/Hazards/DivZero.lean says "A hypothesis `b ≠ 0` in scope does not count (Q3:
conservative v1 contract)", and F02-Q3: "Checker precision is deliberately conservative ...
false positives are cheap (one acknowledgment line) ... tightening is a checker version, recorded
in gate-spec.json by id@version." `divisor?` treats HDiv/HMod, Div/Mod and Nat/Int div and mod
alike, and `syntacticallyNonzero` reads the divisor term alone. And `%` by zero is a real junk
value in Lean (`n % 0 = n`), so the flag is not wrong in general.

Reading: `%` and `/` are treated the same (hole, div_prime: both flagged); a prime hypothesis and a
`p ≠ 0` hypothesis are both ignored, for both operators (mod_ne, div_ne); a literal and `p + 1`
are not flagged (mod_clean). Consistent, and the hypothesis-blind contract of F02-Q3. The cure on
the record is `acknowledged_hazards` (one line), or stating the divisor as `p + 1`. Making the
checker read hypotheses would be a checker version (div-zero@v2) and the owner's call.
