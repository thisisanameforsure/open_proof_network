F02-T14 — a defect claim's exhibit is judged from compiled modules
(audit 2026-10-04, finding A; decisions v3.27 §1; D-16, D-12 v3.23; F08-T17, F08-T21; follows F02-T12)
Branch audit-o-exhibits.

What was wrong
  `opn-gate exhibits` (exhibits.py) judges two kinds of exhibit:
  - every exhibit (a revision request's, a defect claim's of any class): `lean -o` on the text in the
    work directory, after stage_node compiled the node's Context there. The verdict is that compile's
    own exit status and olean, i.e. "it elaborates" (F08-R6). No metaprogram prints a verdict here.
  - a circularity claim's exhibit (class circular-decomposition): additionally
    `opn-relation-type --variant/--root <staged Statement.lean> --relation <Exhibit<n>.lean>`, which
    ELABORATED the exhibit in the process that prints the verdict (initializers on, after the nonce
    is read), with ctx.build_dir on its LEAN_PATH and the work directory mounted writable: the tree
    the exhibit's own compile could write. An #eval in the exhibit can write a nonce-tagged passing
    verdict to the process's stdout (bypassing #eval's own capture) and exit 0. Shown below.

The fix (gate/opn_gate/exhibits.py; RelationTypeMain.lean, toolchain.py and judging.py unchanged)
  check_circular now does what admission's RelationCheck does (F02-T12), with the seams T12 built:
  1. both statements (the hole's and the ancestor's) built from their files of record by
     judging.build_statements in a fresh area <work>.judge/exhibit<n>/meaning, by calls writable only
     there (a Context failure is still `exhibit-node` naming the node; a Statement failure is
     `circular-elaboration`; a definitions failure stands as before);
  2. the exhibit compiled once, apart (judging.compile_contributed: writable only <area>/compile,
     which holds a copy of the trusted build), under a header the gate writes (judging.with_header):
     the union of the two statements' imports plus the exhibit's own. That union is what the
     elaborating program read anyway (unionHeaderEnv over all three headers). Graph modules the
     exhibit may add are what staging compiled for it before: the target's Defs and the Contexts of
     the hole and the ancestor. Anything else (a Statement, another node's module) did not elaborate
     before (no olean was staged) and is now refused by name before compiling (`exhibit-elaboration`);
  3. opn-relation-type in its compiled form (--imports/--variant-olean/--root-olean/--relation-olean),
     through judging.confined(read_only=[build, contributed dir]), with nothing writable and only the
     trusted build on LEAN_PATH: modules of record imported, the exhibit's constants added through
     the kernel by replayInto, nothing of it executed.
  This compile is also the circular exhibit's "it elaborates": one compile, not two. A circular
  claim no longer touches the work directory or stage_node. circular_verdict is unchanged.

  Not converted, and why: the generic exhibit (every non-circular one). Its verdict IS its compile:
  the claim is "this text elaborates", and the only judge of that is the elaborator running the
  text. No compiled form can bind an olean to text. A dishonest exhibit can forge only its own
  admissibility as evidence (e.g. #eval writing an olean and exiting 0), which D-17's adjudicator
  reads in any case. Left as it was; the path is pinned unchanged by test_exhibits.py.

Service side (checked, no change): the api never runs this program. POST /defect-claims and

  sent at all (`relational=True` → PREFLIGHT_SKIPPED). No api module imports opn_gate.exhibits or
  calls relation_type. The variant relation pre-flight (checks.py ~2078) inlines the
  relation program's Lean into AXLE as well. Nothing to convert there.
  Mismatch spotted on the way (not changed, not this task's): checks.EXHIBIT_NODE_MODULES lets an

Residuals (unchanged by this task)
  - The generic exhibit's verdict is its own compile (above).
  - A circular exhibit's compile can still write any olean as its own and exit 0. That olean is
    then replayed through the kernel and judged, so it can only pass if it really proves
    hole → ancestor with allowed axioms. What remains is that the recorded text may not be the text
    judged. (F02-T12's "the text is the module" stance; a proof that exists is still a proof.)
  - Import clashes between two Contexts in the union (diamond deps) fail here exactly as they did
    under unionHeaderEnv.
  - The olean-loader residual of every judge (F02-T12).

  F02-Q14: A defect claim's exhibit is judged from compiled modules (decisions v3.27 §1). Of the
    exhibits `opn-gate exhibits` checks, only a circularity claim's (F08-T17, F08-T21) had a
    verdict printed by a program that elaborated it: opn-relation-type --relation <source>, with

    writable only in that area's scratch directory, under the union of the statements' imports

    Verdicts for honest exhibits are unchanged. Every other exhibit is judged by its own compile,
    because "it elaborates" is the whole claim and no compiled form can stand in for it. A forged
    compile there can make only the exhibit itself admissible as evidence, which D-17 adjudicates
    anyway. The service never runs this program: its pre-flight goes to the hosted checker and
    skips circularity claims.
