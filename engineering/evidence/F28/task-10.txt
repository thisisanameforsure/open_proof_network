                   ¬ (<type>) :=`, binders before the colon bound as `∀ <binders>, <type>`

                   status resolved and the root off the frontier already followed from
                   RESOLVED_STATUSES and in_frontier (asserted)

                   carries the verdict (test_the_sweep_closes_the_root_as_refuted)
