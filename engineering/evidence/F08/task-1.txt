THE JUDGMENT CALL WORTH REVIEWING
---------------------------------
AC5 asks for a variant "labeled partial without Relation.lean". There is no such node. F03
already fixed the convention that a variant's label is the `-- relation:` line inside
Relation.lean, so a claim and its proof are the same file: claiming `partial` and supplying
nothing is not a thing that can exist in the graph. The criterion is met in the two places the
impossibility is actually enforced — the scaffold refuses to build it, and admission refuses a
Relation.lean carrying no label (`relation-unlabelled`). Recorded as F08-Q6.

WHAT THE REAL TOOLCHAIN SAID
----------------------------
Two failures the fast tier could not have caught, both about *imports*:

1. The relation check reads three files — the variant's statement, the root's statement and the
   relation proof — and no one of their headers is a superset of the others'. The metaprogram now
   synthesises a header from the union of the three and imports that once
   (`OpnGate.Frontend.unionHeaderEnv`). `opn-artifact-type` uses it too, for the same reason.

2. The union is not enough on its own: the root's statement imports the root's `Context`, which
   nothing had compiled — the layout check compiles only the Context of the node being admitted.
   The relation check now stages and compiles the root's Context first. Both failures presented
   identically (`relation-elaboration`), which is why the message names what could not be
   imported rather than just saying the file is bad.
