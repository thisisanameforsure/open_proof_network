THE DEFECT
  The post-merge writer creates a hole child with the witness slot (`postmerge.WITNESS_SLOT`,
  formatted with `expected="True"`) and never passes admission: D-29 says a hole is admitted
  without a witness and stays blocked until one is supplied. `curator.revise` copies the old
  node's witness (R9), so a revision of a never-witnessed hole arrived carrying that slot, and
  admission ran step 7 on it and refused `witness-sorry`. Graph PRs #95 and #96 landed only with
  witnesses written by hand; #97 and #98 were closed for want of one. The asymmetry was the gate's:
  the writer may create such a node, a curator may not correct one.

THE FIX
  gate/opn_gate/admit.py     `witness_slot_is_open(node, target_id)` and `WitnessSlotCheck`,
                             registered under the stable name `witness` in `default_checks()`.
                             Four conditions, all required: the node's origin is a hole origin;
                             `supersedes` names a node of the target; that node's own origin is a
                             hole origin; the witness is byte for byte the writer's slot. Then
                             `passed_with("witness-slot-open", origin=..., supersedes=...)` and
                             `ctx.data["witness"] = {"slot": "open", ...}` (C7, never silent);
                             otherwise `WitnessStep().run(ctx)`, step 7 in full. The submission
                             pipeline's own step 7 (`steps/__init__.py`) is not this class.
  gate/opn_gate/curator.py   `revise(..., witness=None)`: the copied witness is the default, a
                             given one replaces it; an empty one is refused by `scaffold.validate`
                             before a byte is written.
  gate/opn_gate/cli.py       `revise --witness <file>`, read like `--statement`.
  docs/architecture_decisions_v_3_12.html   D-29, one clarifying sentence: a revision of such a
                             hole, carrying the slot it inherited, is admitted the same way; the
                             statement changed, not the witness rule. A clarification, not an
                             overturn (D-4 step 7's line is unqualified and stays so).
