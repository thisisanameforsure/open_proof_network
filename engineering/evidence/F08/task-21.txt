## the rule (the owner): circular is literal — you start at the premise and you end at it
The exhibit proves `hole -> ancestor`: any proof of the hole is a proof of the ancestor, so from the
ancestor the decomposition leads to the hole and the hole leads straight back. A hole that is the
ancestor restated passes by `exact`; a strictly easier hole cannot pass unless the ancestor is
proved. D-12's v3.22 chain still holds: a node between, with its siblings proved, implies its
parent and so the ancestor, as the claimed hole does.
