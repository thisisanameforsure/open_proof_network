## what was found (2026-09-23, the nine testers on the calibration targets)
Two merged skeletons produced holes that are no easier than what they were meant to reduce:
erdos-1050--h1-v2--h1 states the root again after a reindexing identity (the sum over n >= 0 of
1/(2^(n+1) - 3) and the sum of 1/(2^(n+3) - 3) differ by the first two terms, -1 and +1), and
erdos-69--h2-v2--h1-v2--h4 is equivalent to the target. Both were published claimable. A D-16
claim could not say so: no class for it, no field naming the statement it circles back to, and the
gate only checked that an exhibit elaborates, never what it proves.

## the shape
- defect-claim/v3 (new, pinned in gate/schemas/HASHES 7c8096b9...): v2 plus the class
  `circular-decomposition` and the field `ancestor` (required for that class by an if/then).
- classifier (modes.check_circular_claim): the ancestor is a node that depends on the claimed node
  transitively, deps read through D-8 revisions (graph.ancestors over effective_deps); an ancestor
  on any other class is refused. Code: circular-ancestor.
- sandbox (exhibits.check_circular, run by `opn-gate exhibits` on the claim's append PR): the
  exhibit declares exactly one theorem (circular-exhibit), elaborates, and opn-relation-type with
  label `resolves` (variant -> root), the ancestor as the variant and the claimed node as the root,
  says its type is defeq to `ancestor -> node` (circular-direction), with no sorryAx
  (circular-sorry) and no axiom outside the allowlist (circular-axiom). No new Lean executable.
- products: graph.load_target reads the merged claim into NodeFacts.circular
  (records.circular_claim); an open node with one is not workable, so off the frontier, and
  graph.json's cause is `circular` (graph/v3's cause is a slug, so no bump). No record is written:
  deleting the claim restores every product byte for byte (test_no_record_is_rewritten).
- api: POST /defect-claims takes `ancestor`, files circular claims at v3 (everything else stays
  v1), refuses a missing/non-ancestor/misplaced ancestor 400 circular-ancestor; POST /claims on a
  circular node answers 409 node-circular naming nodes/<id>/defects/. MCP file_defect_claim passes
  `ancestor` through (D-28 parity).
- site: the node reads "circular: no easier than a statement it was meant to reduce", is not a
  workable state, and its page says "Not claimable" and links the claim file.
