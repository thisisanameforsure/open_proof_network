F04-T51 (Q50): a statement panel's opening formula wraps instead of being cut off
================================================================================

Defect (measured live 2026-10-14, https://openproofnetwork.org/problems/erdos-1094/#node=erdos-1094--h2
at 1440 px): the panel's "What is claimed" headline and each "Split into" hole row rendered the
math-first gloss's opening $$...$$ block in KaTeX display mode, which never breaks a line. In the
~390 px panel the formulas are 393-449 px wide, so the statement's conclusion (==> k <= K) was cut
off. Each sat in a .katex-display box with overflow-x: auto (site.css), which scrolls with a
trackpad but draws no bar on macOS until scrolled: as the reader sees it, cut off.

Fix: render.breakable_opening turns the opening <span class="math">$$X$$</span> of a rendered gloss
into <span class="math math-break">$\displaystyle X$</span> (whitespace collapsed; a block holding
a lone $ is left alone). Inline math under \displaystyle is set the same, and KaTeX breaks it after
a top-level relation or binary operator. Applied where a gloss's opening formula is shown under a
statement: the claim headline (claim_block, panel and statement page), the split rows
(hole_statement), the whole gloss in the record details (gloss_block) and each gloss version in the
history (version_words, sectionless versions). .math-break is a block, overflow-x: auto; math.js
marks a box .math-overflows when its formula still overflows (a \left...\right group cannot break),
re-measured by a ResizeObserver so a panel or fold shown later is marked too, and the stylesheet
draws a 6 px bar on a marked box. Stored records unchanged; display math anywhere else in prose
(convention notes, explainers, annexes) unchanged.
