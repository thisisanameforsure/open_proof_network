What was built
  Home: hero, the counts card (explained / proved leads; problems, open statements, distinct stewards),
  "Open now" (up to five: unproved first, then proved-but-unexplained), the two cards, the provenance bar.
  Problems: Targets and Frontier merged — one card per target (status tag, fidelity tag, informal, source
  line, stage marks, steward line) over one row per statement (dot, id, role, state, attempts, action);
  the segmented control and search filter client-side (problems.js), the options are plain links carrying
  ?filter= without it; the legend's chips are hover cards; on mobile the legend is a <details>.
  Problem page (/problems/<id>/): breadcrumb, monospace id with the tags, informal, provenance line, stage
  marks, claimability sentence; the steward card; the statement graph as pills sized to their labels with
  one "Selected statement" panel per node (problem.js selects on click, keeps #node=<id>; without it every
  pill is a link and the root's panel shows); the record's detail sections below with rewritten headings.
  About: the former home lead verbatim but for the handoff's two edits, the four rules, the commitment card.
  Docs: the Glossary at /docs/#glossary, rendered from the same GLOSSARY dict as every hover card.
  Statement record and Contributors: kept, breadcrumb and words renamed. /targets/, /frontier/ and
  /targets/<id>/ render as meta-refresh redirects inside the frame. site.css rewritten to the Nocturne
  dark tokens; frontier.js retired.
