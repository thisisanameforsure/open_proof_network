Rule as built (gate/opn_gate/modes.py check_model_lock, sections.breaches):
- A gloss or explainer version naming drafted_with is checked against what its chain shows
  over the versions AHEAD of it (sections.of_chain on the chain prefix, with the valid
  signatures on those versions). Every shown section whose state is written or verified and
  whose text the new version changes or omits is one `locked-by-a-person` diagnostic.
- The diagnostic names the section key, its state, the version whose words are shown, the
  Lean file relative to the target, and for an explainer's steps: section the line span
  [min start_line, max end_line] over its steps in targets/<t>/outlines/<proof-hash>.json.
  An overview section, a gloss, a missing outline or a missing step: lines null, "all of <file>".
- A person's version (drafted_with null, no drafter block) is never refused by the lock.
- A version starting a chain of its own has nothing ahead of it and is never locked.
- A legacy drafter block is refused draft-not-accepted before the lock runs (R2).
- Runs in modes.check after record-not-head, so the service's pre-flight
  (modes.check_as_service) reaches it with no change of its own (route case below).
