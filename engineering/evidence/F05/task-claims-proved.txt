F05 — a claim on a proved node is released (owner, 2026-10-28: "it should be released after proven")
=====================================================================================================

Finding: erdos-68, 2026-10-28 — agent B's claims on erdos-68--h1/--h2 were still listed active
1h20 after both proofs merged. A claim lived until its TTL whatever happened to its node: it
counted against the cap, appeared in GET /claims/mine, named its holder in others' receipts
(`others`), and reached the public registry (claims.json, /frontier.json overlay).

Rule built: lazy, like expiry. frontier.live(ctx, claims) = unreleased, not past expires, and
not on a node its target's committed graph.json calls `proved`. Used by active_for (the cap and
/claims/mine), claims.others, and frontier.registry (/claims.json and the /frontier.json
overlay). Nothing is written; a proof-released claim stops being listed exactly as an expired
one does, its `released` stays null, and history_count still counts it. Only `proved` releases
(blocked, abandoned, superseded and shelved are pinned unchanged by test). The graph is read
through the committed-file cache once per target that has a candidate claim, and its proved-id
set is parsed once per body (functools.lru_cache keyed on the cached bytes); a graph that cannot
be read releases nothing (C7).
