carrying the per-call nonce (32 hex, secrets.token_hex(16)), so a contributor's own logInfo of the

4. Decisions
------------
- Order of refusals: fields (node fields refused by name; automation; pair; lean) -> target ->
  review-unknown -> candidate-unknown / pair-candidate-not-surviving -> pair-unknown /
  pair-direction-settled (record in force; then a merged pair-proof record for the direction whose
  day is >= the pair record's date, i.e. one the job has not answered) -> pair-proof-pending ->
  pair-proof-no-template -> pair-proof-template-mismatch / pair-proof-forbidden -> rate limit ->
  pre-flight (pair-proof-fails, -sorry, -axiom, -degenerate). Cheap refusals cost no rate slot; a
  pre-flighted attempt does.
- Rate limit: 10 per pseudonym per target per day (candidates: 3). A candidate is a new reading
  every later pair compares; a settlement closes work and is the intended activity, but costs up to
  two hosted checks (also charged to the hourly check budget, reserved together, F13-T28), and
  iteration belongs on POST /check.
- Disclosure: the route's own `automation` (required, check_automation, one vocabulary with
  submit_proof); the record's `models` is automation.models verbatim; the submission is recorded
  with its automation so last_method sees it.
- Axioms: the standard three (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound), not the target's
  axiom_allowlist, as the brief says; refused sorryAx first (pair-proof-sorry), then any other
  (pair-proof-axiom). No axiom line (an older checker, a file that stops early) is `inconclusive`,
  unless the checker's own warning says sorry.
- Words in the receipt (`pair_proof_preflight`): passed | inconclusive | unavailable | skipped (the
  file imports a non-library module only the gate's staging supplies, the exhibit precedent).
- Blind rule (F26-R3, "the service, the MCP and the site all hold to it"): a template is both
  candidates' Lean, so `template` and the refutation template are shown only to a caller whose
  pseudonym authored a candidate on the record (the same rule GET /reviews applies to `lean`);
  otherwise template_withheld: blind. Proposed as a Q (a prover who wrote no candidate cannot get a
  template from the service).
- Refutation templates: `n` on list_open_pairs and get_review (and ?n= on GET /reviews/<id>).
- MCP: submit_proof's required arguments are now artifact_type and automation (node_id and bundle
  are refused by the route when missing, node-id-missing / bundle-invalid, as before); a
  pair-proof call forwards every argument and the route refuses node fields by name.
  list_open_pairs and get_review result schemas at v2 (results.VERSIONS), v1 kept.
- tools/list budget re-pinned 113,000 -> 115,000 on a measurement: 112,871 at the contract commit
  ebd36797 (detached worktree), 114,654 after (+1,783, 1.6%).
- Codes: ten new, source api. pair-proof-template-mismatch and pair-proof-forbidden stay source
  gate: the service relays the gate's own Diagnostic (a RELAYS row in test_error_codes.py, the
  checks.py/glosses.py precedent); making them `both` failed test_source_matches_... because the
  walk sees only the gate constructing them.
