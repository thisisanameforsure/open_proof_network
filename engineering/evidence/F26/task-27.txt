== Cause and fix ==
Cause: pair_proof_check.admissible (called by modes' pair-proof check from `classify`, which the
post-merge job and every replay run on the merge) refused any direction whose pair record in force
is not `open`. A replay reads main as it is, where the review job had already accepted this very
record and written the pair as proved from it, so the record was refused as closed against itself.
Fix: when the review job's judgment of this record's own stem (review.pair_proof_judgments) is an
acceptance, and the pair in force names that judgment's exhibit with its result, the record is
already judged: the open and `pair-proof-settled` rules are skipped; the template checks still run.
A direction closed by any other record or exhibit is refused `pair-not-open` exactly as before.
