F14-T23 — the witness pass over every refused row, and the four draft defects it found
=====================================================================================

The pass. 282 rows refused for their witness alone (T21's `--write-refused` wrote their statements),
eight agents, one hour each, the hosted checker as the pre-flight. The checker's anonymous budget
(200 calls a day, shared by every lane and the pilot) ran out eleven calls into the pass, so the lanes
were told to write every remaining witness against the statement's binders unchecked; the gate's
admission (T20) is the judge, as it was always going to be. Result: 281 Witness.lean files written
(the pilot's 19 among them), none with `sorry`; 12 rows the agents declined with a reason — the
hypothesis is itself a theorem or an open question (the Ulam sequence, Mahler's Z-numbers, a
nontrivial zero of an L-function, Schoenberg's density theorem, an NP-complete problem), or a
construction too large to write blind (a 2-colouring of the plane with no monochromatic copy of two
triangles, Bondy's cycle, Sylvester's sequence summable in ℚ). Reports: task-23-pass/lane-{0..7}.md.
The witnesses are the curator's inputs to the driver, committed under
engineering/onramp/open/bulk/overrides/<id>/Witness.lean (281) so the admission workflow reads them.

Four draft defects the lanes read off the statements, each a test before its fix (test_wave.py):
  section variables    the registry states theorems over `variable {α : Type*} [Fintype α] …`; the
                       draft carried the theorem's own binders only, so `α` auto-bound and `minDegree`
                       lacked its `Fintype` (conjecture84, conjecture40, eisenbud-green-harris, …).
                       The variables the statement uses, and the instances over them, are now its
                       binders before the colon, in the file's order; the unused ones are not.
  local notations      `local notation "F(" s ", " n ")"`, `𝔽₂`, `(!n)`, `≪`: not names, so the token
                       scan never saw them and the statement did not parse (erdos-561, -749, kurepa,
                       balister-gyori-schelp). A notation the statement uses is ported into Local.lean
                       as a plain notation (a `local` one reaches nothing outside its module); T11's
                       refusal is gone.
  Greek identifiers    the identifier scan was ASCII (`[A-Za-z_]`), so `α`, `ε`, `κ` were never names:
                       no variable over them was found. `\w`, Unicode-aware, with no leading digit.
  the file's own names the catalog's `local_defs` missed some (`IsGoormaghtighNumber` was in a
                       binder; T21 fixed the binder scan), and the driver now reads the file's own
                       declarations too, so a name the catalog missed is still ported.
Red first: 4 of 40 tests (the two count tests, the variables test, the notation test); the Unicode
fix was found by the variables test staying red after the code (`α` was not a token). Green: 40
passed; ruff, ruff format, mypy --strict clean; the workflow passes the action-pin and concurrency
rules and parses.
