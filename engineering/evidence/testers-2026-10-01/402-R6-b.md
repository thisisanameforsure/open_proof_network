### `hlarge` (n > 200014): statement and what could close it
Statement: exactly your `Tlarge` (node hypotheses → 200014 < B.card → pair ∨ GEN(B.card)). Witness: 402-R5-a's
`holes/h2-hlarge-witness.lean` (B = Icc 1 200016) fits unchanged apart from the node id.
Honest assessment: **no known explicit (or inexplicit) prime-gap theorem closes it through the window disjunct.**
- Width of the generalised window, from GenCriterion's hypotheses: every α in (p − n, n) must have no x < y with
  x·y ∣ α and α·y < n·x (unless a prime q ≥ n/3 divides α(p − α)). Taking y = x + 1, α = n − s: α is bad as soon as it
  has a divisor x(x+1) with α/s < x, which forces s > about √n and is met by the pronic number x(x+1) nearest below
  n − √n. So the first bad α sits at distance s1(n) with √n ≤ s1(n) ≤ about 3√n (argument: sketch, not Lean;
  numerics `width.py`/`width.out`: s1/√n has min 1.00, median 1.8, max 2.98 on 1,500 random n in 10³..2·10⁶).
  The window prime must therefore satisfy 2n − p ≤ s1(n) ≤ 3√n: a prime in an interval of length O(√x) below x = 2n.
- The q-escape (a prime ≥ n/3 dividing α(p − α)) skips a bad α only when p − α or α is q or 2q; it rescued 678..680
  once but has density about 1/log n and gives no systematic widening. (Heuristic, not proved.)
- A Bertrand-type result "a prime in (x, x(1 + 1/k)] for x ≥ x0" gives a gap x/k; it is ≤ 3√(x/2) only for
  x ≤ about 4.5·k². So any fixed k covers a FINITE range only (k = 16597, Schoenfeld's x0 = 2,010,760: up to about
  1.2·10⁹ at best, and only where the window constant really is near 3, which it is not for all n). Dusart-type
  x/(c·log²x) gaps fail the same way. Unconditional gaps are x^0.525 (Baker–Harman–Pintz, inexplicit); even RH gives
  only O(√x·log x). O(√x) is Legendre-conjecture territory: open.
- So `hlarge` cannot be closed by the window disjunct with known mathematics; it must be closed through its first
  disjunct (a good pair for large sets) by a different argument. From memory, unverified here: the conjecture was
  proved for large n by Szegedy (1986) and Zaharescu (1987) and for all n by Balasubramanian–Soundararajan (1996);
  those proofs are not window-prime arguments. I have not checked what they need; treat this as a pointer, not a plan.
  Suggest the annex on `hlarge` says exactly this, so nobody spends a round on prime-gap tables.
