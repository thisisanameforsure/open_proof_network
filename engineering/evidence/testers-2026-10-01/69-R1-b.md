## Bugs
1. `POST /check` with `"mode":"hazards"` is broken on erdos-69 (probably every lean-4.33.1 target). Repro:
   `curl -s -X POST $OPN_API/check -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"target_id":"erdos-69","mode":"hazards","node_id":"spec-2e765953"}'`
   → `okay:false, hazards:null, user_error:null`; `result.lean_messages.errors` are in the service's own injected runner:
   `Unknown identifier Lean.Elab.IO.processCommands` (and `Invalid field notation ... st.commandState`). Same with a
   `statement` instead of `node_id` (log_id 01M3VN65W8FT3VSMP1V8K3BWSR). The caller cannot tell this from a fault of its own
   statement; and the proposal routes' `hazards_preflight` presumably reads `inconclusive` for everyone. Severity: medium.
2. `POST /check` `"mode":"hazards"` with `content` (and no `statement`/`node_id`) answers `400 node-id-required`, fine, but the
   guide's sentence "with a node_id or a statement" is easy to miss next to every other mode taking `content`. Low.
3. `POST /annexes` refuses the disclosure field under the name `/submissions` uses: sending `tooling` → `400 unknown-field`
   ("accepts licence, model_and_tooling, node_id, text"). The guide's annex example shows neither field. The error message is
   good; the two names for one thing are the bug. Low.
4. Record content (not the service): annex 99dda822 on erdos-69 understates the external proof's closure (misses
   Erdos697PrimeHarmonic and the FormalPantheon dependency) and calls it the Tao–Teräväinen route; the external proof is a
   different, elementary argument. Corrected in annex 9445873d.

## Feature requests
1. A contributor route to propose `defs/` for a target (reviewed like a statement). It is the single blocker for porting any
   proof whose hard lemma needs vocabulary: here ~100 definitions, without which the two real holes cannot be stated and
   every statable hole is circular.
2. Multi-file fast check (a bundle of modules, or `content` as a list in dependency order) with a per-module verdict. To test
   a 50-module development I had to concatenate, wrap each module in `section … end` (top-level `open`s leak: `Ioc`
   ambiguous) and sorry-strip dependencies to stay under 20 s / 200 kB.
3. A licence field on annexes that can say "contains Apache-2.0 material from X" (or per-block licences). An annex is CC-BY-4.0
   only, so attributed Apache-2.0 Lean cannot be put in one cleanly; I left that half out of annex b8402c93.
4. When the hazards runner itself fails, answer `hazards: "unavailable"` with a service-fault flag instead of `okay:false`.
5. `/check` could return `#print axioms` for the last theorem by default (I appended it by hand).
