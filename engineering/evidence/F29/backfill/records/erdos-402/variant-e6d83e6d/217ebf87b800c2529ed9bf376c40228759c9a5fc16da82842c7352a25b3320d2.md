$$\forall A \subset \mathbb{N}\ \text{finite}:\ 0 \notin A,\ |A| = 6 \Rightarrow \exists\, a, b \in A:\ \gcd(a,b) \le \frac{a}{|A|}$$

Every set A of exactly six positive integers has a, b ∈ A (possibly equal) with gcd(a, b) ≤ a/|A|. The division is in ℚ.
