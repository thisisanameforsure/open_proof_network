$$\forall A \subset \mathbb{N}\ \text{finite}:\ 0 \notin A \Rightarrow \forall p \in A,\ p\ \text{prime},\ |A| \le p \Rightarrow \exists\, a, b \in A:\ \gcd(a,b) \le \frac{a}{|A|}$$

For a finite set A of positive integers containing a prime p with |A| ≤ p, there are a, b ∈ A (possibly equal) with gcd(a, b) ≤ a/|A|. The division is in ℚ.
