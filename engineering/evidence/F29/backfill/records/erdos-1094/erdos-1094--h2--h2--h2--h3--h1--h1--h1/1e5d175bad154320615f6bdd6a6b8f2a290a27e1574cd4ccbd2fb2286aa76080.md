$$65536\,X'<1\ \Longrightarrow\ |S'|<65536\,N'X'+2r\lambda',\qquad X'=(D'\lambda'^{\,r}W^2)^{\frac{1}{2r-1}}+\Bigl(\frac{\delta W^2}{D'\lambda'^{\,r}}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{r-1}}+\Bigl(\frac{D_1'\lambda' W^2}{D'}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{2r}}$$

Fix $r\ge 2$, $W\ge 1$, $\delta>0$ and the data of the enclosing statement ($N>0$, $\lambda\ge 1$, $D_r,D_{r+1}>0$, $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ of class $C^{r+1}$ with $D_r/2\le|f^{(r)}(x)|\le D_r$ and $|f^{(r+1)}(x)|\le D_{r+1}$ for all $x\in[0,N]$, every finite set $S$ of integers in $[0,\lfloor N\rfloor]$ each of whose points $u$ has $|f(u)-v/w|<\delta$ for some integer $v$ and natural $w$ with $0<w\le W$), and assume moreover $\neg(1\le 65536\,X)$ for $X$ the same three-term expression in $D_r,\lambda,D_{r+1}$. Then for every $N'>0$, every $\lambda'$ with $1\le\lambda'<2$, every $D'>0$ and $D_1'>0$, every $g:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ of class $C^{r+1}$ with $D'/2\le|g^{(r)}|\le D'$ and $|g^{(r+1)}|\le D_1'$ on $[0,N']$, and every finite set $S'$ of integers in $[0,\lfloor N'\rfloor]$ each of whose points $u$ has $|g(u)-v/w|<\delta$ for some integer $v$ and natural $0<w\le W$, the displayed bound holds. The hypotheses on $N$, $\lambda$, $D_r$, $D_{r+1}$, $f$ and $S$ are carried from the enclosing statement and play no part in the conclusion; the Lean names $\lambda'$ `lam'` and $D_1'$ `D1'`, $W^2$ is a natural power and the fractional exponents are real powers.
