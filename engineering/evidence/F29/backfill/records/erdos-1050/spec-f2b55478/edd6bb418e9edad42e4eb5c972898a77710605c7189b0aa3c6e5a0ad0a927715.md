$$\forall n<m:\quad \sum_{j=0}^{n}\frac{(-1)^j\,2^{j(j-1)/2}\prod_{i=0}^{j-1}\frac{2^{\,n-i}-1}{2^{\,i+1}-1}\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{2^{\,2n-j-i}-1}{2^{\,i+1}-1}}{2^{\,m-j}-1}
\;=\;\frac{2^{n^2}\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(2^{\,i+1}-1)\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{2^{\,m-n-1-i}-1}{2^{\,i+1}-1}}{\prod_{j=0}^{n}(2^{\,m-j}-1)}$$

For natural numbers $n<m$ this identity holds in $\mathbb{Q}$; the numerator of the $j$-th term on the left is $Q_c(n,j)$ as the Padé family defines it, so the left side is $\sum_{j=0}^{n}Q_c(n,j)/(2^{m-j}-1)$. All subtractions in exponents are natural-number subtraction, which stops at $0$, and $j(j-1)/2$ is natural-number division: since $j\le n<m$ no denominator is $0$ and $n-i$, $2n-j-i$ never truncate, while $m-n-1-i$ does when $m\le 2n$ (the factor $i=m-n-1$ is then $2^0-1=0$ and both sides are $0$). The doc comment's reading as a power-series coefficient, and its sign claim for $m>2n$, are not part of the statement.
