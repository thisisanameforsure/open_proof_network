$$\exists\, a,b:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{Z}\ \ \bigl(\forall n\in\mathbb{N},\ b_n\,T-a_n\neq 0\bigr)\ \wedge\ \lim_{n\to\infty}\bigl(b_n\,T-a_n\bigr)=0,\qquad T=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^{m+3}-3}$$

There are two sequences of integers $a_n$, $b_n$ such that $b_n T - a_n$ is never zero and converges to $0$ in $\mathbb{R}$ (`Filter.Tendsto … Filter.atTop (nhds 0)`), where $T$ is the real number $\sum_{m\ge 0} 1/(2^{m+3}-3) = \tfrac15+\tfrac1{13}+\tfrac1{29}+\cdots$. The sum is Lean's `∑'` over all natural numbers (the file also calls the summation variable `n`; it is a separate bound variable), which is $0$ for a non-summable family, and $a_n$, $b_n$ are read as real numbers; no hypothesis is assumed and irrationality is not mentioned.
