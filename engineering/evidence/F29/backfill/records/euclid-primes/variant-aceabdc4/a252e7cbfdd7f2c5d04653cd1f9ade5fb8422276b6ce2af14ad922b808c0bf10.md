$$\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\ \ \exists p\in\mathbb{N}:\quad n<p\ \wedge\ 100\le p\ \wedge\ p\ \text{prime}$$

For every natural number $n$ there is a prime $p$ with $p>n$ and $p\ge 100$. Prime is the problem's own `Opn.IsPrime`: $2\le p$ and every $d$ with $p=d\cdot c$ for some natural $c$ is $1$ or $p$.
