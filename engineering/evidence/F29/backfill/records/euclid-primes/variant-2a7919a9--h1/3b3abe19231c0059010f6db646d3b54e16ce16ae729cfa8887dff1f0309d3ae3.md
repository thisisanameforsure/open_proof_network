$$\forall\, l\in\operatorname{List}(\mathbb{N}),\ \forall x\in l:\quad x\le \sum l$$

Every element of a list of natural numbers is at most the sum of the list (`List.sum`, the empty list summing to $0$). There are no other hypotheses.
