$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigl[n\ \text{prime}\bigr]\Bigl(\frac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{n} \notin\mathbb{Q}$$

The real number whose binary expansion has a 1 in place n exactly when n is prime, the sum over n ≥ 0 of (1/2)^n for prime n and 0 otherwise, is irrational. The series is a tsum over ℝ (0 when not summable), and `Irrational x` means x is not the cast of a rational.
