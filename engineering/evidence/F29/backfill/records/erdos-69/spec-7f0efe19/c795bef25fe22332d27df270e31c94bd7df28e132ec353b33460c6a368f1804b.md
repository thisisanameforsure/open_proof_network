$$\forall k\in\mathbb{N},\ \forall c:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}\ \ \exists N\in\mathbb{N}\ \ \forall j<k:\quad c(j) \le \omega(N+1+j)$$

For every length k and every sequence of naturals c there is a natural N with c(j) ≤ ω(N+1+j) for each j < k, where ω(n) is the number of distinct prime factors of n.
