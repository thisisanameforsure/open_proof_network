$$\forall b\in\mathbb{N},\ b\ge 2:\quad \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{b^{p}} \notin\mathbb{Q}$$

For every natural base b ≥ 2, the sum over the primes p of 1/b^p, in ℝ, is irrational. The series is a tsum over ℝ (0 when not summable), and `Irrational x` means x is not the cast of a rational.
