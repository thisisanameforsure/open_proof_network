$$\forall M\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0 < \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(M+1+j)}{2^{j+1}}$$

Under the hypotheses that the Lambert identity ∑_{n≥0} ω(n)/2^n = ∑_p 1/(2^p − 1) holds and that every rescaled tail splits as T(N) = ∑_{j<k} ω(N+1+j)/2^{j+1} + T(N+k)/2^k, the tail T(M) is strictly positive for every natural M. Here T(M) denotes the rescaled tail ∑_{j≥0} ω(M+1+j)/2^{j+1}, a tsum over ℝ (0 if not summable), and ω(n) is the number of distinct prime factors of n.
