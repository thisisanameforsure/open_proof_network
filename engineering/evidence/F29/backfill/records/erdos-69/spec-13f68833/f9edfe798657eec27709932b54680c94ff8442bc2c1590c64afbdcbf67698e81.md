$$\forall n\in\mathbb{N},\ C\in\mathbb{R}:\quad \Bigl(\forall k\in\mathbb{N},\ \omega(n+1+k) \le C\,(k+1)\Bigr) \Rightarrow \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(n+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} \le 2C$$

If, for a natural n and a real C, ω(n+1+k) ≤ C(k+1) for every natural k, then the tail ∑_{j≥0} ω(n+1+j)/2^{j+1} is at most 2C, with ω(n) the number of distinct prime factors of n. Every series is Mathlib's `tsum` over ℝ, which is 0 when the family is not summable.
