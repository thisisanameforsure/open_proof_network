$$\forall m\ge 3:\quad \bigl(\neg\,\exists x,\ 2\le x<m\ \wedge\ \exists k,\ \sigma^{[k]}(x)=m\bigr)\ \Rightarrow\ \exists m',\ 2\le m'<m\ \wedge\ \exists i,j,\ \sigma^{[i]}(m)=\sigma^{[j]}(m')$$

For every natural number $m\ge 3$: if no $x$ with $2\le x<m$ reaches $m$ under some iterate of $\sigma$, then there is an $m'$ with $2\le m'<m$ whose forward $\sigma$-orbit meets that of $m$, that is, $\sigma^{[i]}(m)=\sigma^{[j]}(m')$ for some $i,j\ge 0$. Here $\sigma=\sigma_1$ is Mathlib's sum-of-divisors function on $\mathbb{N}$ (`ArithmeticFunction.sigma 1`, the `σ` notation opened in the file) and $\sigma^{[k]}$ its $k$-fold iterate, with $\sigma^{[0]}$ the identity; the variables are natural numbers by inference.
