Erdős's tail argument: split the rescaled tail after k terms, bound the remainder by log₂(N+k+1) + 1, and choose N, k so that b times the tail lands strictly between two integers.

The holes are `hsplit` (the tail is a finite head plus the next tail over 2^k), `hpos` and `hbound` (the tail is positive and at most log₂(M+1) + 1), and `hcrux` (some N, k with the residue of b·(head·2^k) mod 2^k plus b·(log₂(N+k+1) + 1) below 2^k). The assembly multiplies b times the tail by 2^k and shows it lies strictly between q·2^k and (q+1)·2^k, so it equals no integer z.
