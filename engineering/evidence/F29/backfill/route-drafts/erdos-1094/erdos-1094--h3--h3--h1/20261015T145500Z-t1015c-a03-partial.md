Chebyshev's sum over (k/2, 11k/20) from the prime number theorem: θ(x) = x + o(x) at x = 11k/20 and x = k/2 gives Σ log p ≥ k/100 for large k.

The one hole is `h_pnt`: for every ε > 0, |θ(x) − x| ≤ εx for all large x. The assembly applies it with ε = 1/100 at A = (11k − 1)/20 and B = k/2, bounds θ(A) − θ(B) by the sum over the primes p < k with k < 2p and 20p < 11k, and finishes by arithmetic.
