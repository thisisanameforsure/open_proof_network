Residue form split by parity: k even, or k and n both odd (k odd with n even carries at p = 2 and is closed in the assembly).

The holes are `h_keven` (k ≡ 0 mod 2) and `h_odd` (k ≡ n ≡ 1 mod 2). The assembly takes the larger bound with k > 1 and, for k odd and n even, answers with p = 2, since n mod 2 = 0 < 1 = k mod 2.
