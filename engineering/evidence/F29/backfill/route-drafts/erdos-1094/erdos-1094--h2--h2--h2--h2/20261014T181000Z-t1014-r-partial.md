Konyagin's count on the medium range: few m in (k, k + k^θ) have n/m near an integer, so BHP's primes leave one that divides (n−k)⋯(n−1).

The one hole is `h_count`, the bound 3 + 56k^(2θ−1) + k^θ(log k)^(−2) on the m in (k, k + k^θ) with n/m within k^(θ−1) of an integer, for k^(2−θ)/2 < n ≤ k²/log²k and θ = 21/40. The assembly proves that a window prime with n/p far from every integer divides ∏(n − i) (`dvd_from_far`), that the bound is eventually below C k^θ/log k (`med_rhs_le`), and closes by pigeonhole against BHP's prime count (`konyagin_finish`).
