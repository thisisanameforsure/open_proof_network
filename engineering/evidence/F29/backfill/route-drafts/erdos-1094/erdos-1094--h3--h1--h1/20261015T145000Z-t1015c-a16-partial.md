Residue form with k even, split by whether some prime q ≤ k divides n but not k: such a q carries at once, so the hole is the case where every prime ≤ k dividing n divides k.

The one hole is `h_smooth`: the residue form under the extra hypothesis that every prime q ≤ k dividing n divides k. The assembly closes the other case directly, since q ∣ n and q ∤ k give n mod q = 0 < k mod q.
