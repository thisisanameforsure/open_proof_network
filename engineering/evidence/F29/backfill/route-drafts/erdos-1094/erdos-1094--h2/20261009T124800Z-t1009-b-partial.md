Split n ≥ k² at n = k³: bounded k on k² ≤ n < k³ and bounded k on n ≥ k³, each its own hole.

The holes are `h_lo` (k² ≤ n < k³) and `h_hi` (k³ ≤ n), each a bound K on k when the least prime factor of C(n,k) exceeds n/k. The assembly takes the larger of the two bounds and decides by whether n < k³.
