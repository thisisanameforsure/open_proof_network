Sander's equidistribution from GR's Lemma 5.1: the log-weight of primes p with {n/p} < σ is σ Σ log p + o(k), so averaging over σ = j/M gives Σ({n/p} − 1/2) log p = o(k).

The one hole is `h_l51`, the counting form at every σ in [0, 1] with error δk. The assembly bounds Σ log p over (k/2, 11k/20) by 2k (`h_theta`), approximates {n/p} by its step average over σ = j/M with M ≈ 4/ε (`hA`, `hswap`), sums the lemma over j (`hgsum`, `hE`) and brackets the target between the two averages.
