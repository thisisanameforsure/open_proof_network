$$\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\ \ \exists p\in\mathbb{N}:\quad n<p\ \wedge\ \bigl(\exists k\in\mathbb{N},\ p=100+k\bigr)\ \wedge\ p\ \text{prime}$$

For every natural number $n$ there is a prime $p>n$ that is at least $100$, the bound being written as "$p=100+k$ for some natural number $k$" rather than as $100\le p$. Prime is the problem's own `Opn.IsPrime`: $2\le p$ and every $d$ with $p=d\cdot c$ for some natural $c$ is $1$ or $p$.
