$$\{\,p\in\mathbb{N}\ :\ p\ \text{prime}\,\}\ \text{is infinite}$$

The set of natural numbers that are prime is infinite, in Mathlib's sense `Set.Infinite` (not a finite set). Prime is the problem's own `Opn.IsPrime`: $2\le p$ and every $d$ with $p=d\cdot c$ for some natural $c$ is $1$ or $p$.
