$$\forall A \subset \mathbb{N}\ \text{finite},\ \forall m \in \mathbb{N}:\ 0 \notin A,\ \bigl(\forall a, b \in A,\ a \le m \cdot \gcd(a,b)\bigr) \Rightarrow \forall a, b \in A\ \exists\, u, v \in \mathbb{N}:\ 0 < u \le m,\ 0 < v \le m,\ \gcd(u,v) = 1,\ a v = b u$$

For a finite set A of positive integers and a natural number m, if a ≤ m·gcd(a, b) for all a, b ∈ A, then every pair a, b ∈ A satisfies a·v = b·u for some coprime u, v with 1 ≤ u, v ≤ m (the quotient a/b is a reduced fraction u/v of order m). Everything is over ℕ; a and b may coincide.
