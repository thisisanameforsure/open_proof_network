$$\forall P,j\in\mathbb{N},\ 2\le P,\ j\le P:\quad \sum_{p\mid 1+P\#\,(1+j)}\frac{1}{p} \le \frac{5}{\log P}$$

For naturals P ≥ 2 and j ≤ P, the sum of 1/p over the prime factors p of 1 + P#·(1+j) is at most 5/log P, where P# is the primorial (the product of the primes at most P), the sum runs over `(…).primeFactors`, and log is the natural logarithm, all in ℝ.
