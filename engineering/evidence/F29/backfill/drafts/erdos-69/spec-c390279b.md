$$\exists p>0,\ N_0\ \ \forall n,n'\ge N_0,\ n\equiv n' \pmod p\ \ \exists z\in\mathbb{Z}:\quad \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{f(n+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} - \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{f(n'+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} = z$$

For every f : ℕ → ℕ and rational q, if the family f(n)/2^n is summable in ℝ and q equals its sum ∑_{n≥0} f(n)/2^n, then there are a positive natural p and a natural N₀ such that any two tails taken from n, n' ≥ N₀ with n ≡ n' (mod p) differ by an integer. The tails are tsums over ℝ (0 when not summable); the hypotheses are the "if" part and the display is the conclusion.
