$$\forall c>0\ \exists K_3\in\mathbb{N}\ \forall n,k\in\mathbb{N}:\quad K_3<k,\ \ k^{1+c}<n<k^{7/4} \implies \exists\,p \text{ prime},\ \tfrac{k}{2}<p<k,\ \ p\mid\tbinom{n}{k}$$

For every real $c>0$ there is a threshold $K_3$ such that for all $k>K_3$ and all $n$ strictly between $k^{1+c}$ and $k^{7/4}$, some prime $p$ with $k<2p$ and $p<k$ divides $\binom{n}{k}$. The range hypotheses are real inequalities on the casts of $k$ and $n$ with real exponents `(1 + c)` and `(7 / 4 : ℝ)`; the conclusion is in $\mathbb{N}$, with $k/2<p$ written as `k < 2 * p`.
