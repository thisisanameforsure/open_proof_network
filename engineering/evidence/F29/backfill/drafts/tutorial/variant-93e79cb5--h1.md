$$\forall\, p\,q:\operatorname{Prop},\quad p\wedge q\ \Rightarrow\ q\wedge p$$

For any two propositions $p$ and $q$, if $p$ and $q$ both hold then $q$ and $p$ both hold. There are no other hypotheses.
