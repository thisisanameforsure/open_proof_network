WHY
  products.py `in_frontier`: admitted anything `graph.awaiting_witness` accepts, and that asks only
  whether the witness is a stub and the origin is a hole, never whether the node has a record
  (graph.py `blocked_because` is override-blind). The only elaboration in the products is the
  library-tag scan; since 0401858 `TagCache.tags` swallows the failure, writes no entry, and the
  caller cannot tell "elaborated, no Mathlib namespace" from "did not elaborate". And the scan ran
  as an argument expression after membership was decided.

THE CHANGE
  gate/opn_gate/graph.py     `_is_superseded` promoted to `is_superseded` (its one caller,
                             `find_root`, updated).
  gate/opn_gate/products.py  `workable(status, node, status_of)`: superseded -> False; ready or
                             speculative -> True; else `blocked` and awaiting its witness. One
                             predicate for `in_frontier` and for `frontier_entry`'s `claimable`,
                             so the two cannot drift (the 2026-09-14 lesson). `in_frontier` guards
                             on `is_superseded` first, which also drops a superseded variant.
                             `TagCache.failed`, filled where the scan raises, never written to
                             disk. The entry loop scans before it appends and leaves out a node
                             whose scan raised, with a warning naming target, node and reason.
  Not built: a `cause` for the scan failure in `graph.json` (it renders before the scan; the fact
  would have to flow backwards) — Q13.

WHAT THE TESTS ASSERT
  - premise then rule: `awaiting_witness` still says yes for a superseded hole (it cannot tell);
    `in_frontier` says no, for the superseded status and for a stale `blocked` map alike; the same
    hole with no record is still on the frontier (T9 stands);
  - `frontier_entry` says `claimable: false` for it and `true` for the live hole;
  - a stale, disputed or abandoned hole is off the frontier; a speculative node is on;
  - the variant rule is untouched: an abandoned variant is listed (AC14, Q12), a superseded one
    is not;
  - end to end over a tree, in the live shape (hole, its meta/v4 revision with `supersedes`, the
    curator writer's record): old hole absent, successor present and claimable, `graph.json` still
    carries the old node as `superseded`, `derive_causes` gives None vs `witness-missing` (the
    api's view), the frontier validates;
  - a raising scanner on a Mathlib-pinned fixture: the node absent, the others' tags kept, no
    cache entry for it, both warnings logged, its `graph.json` status untouched, and the next
    render scans it again (no negative cache);
  - a Mathlib-free graph never scans, so the frontier is byte-identical with or without a scanner;
  - `test_tag_cache_survives_one_nodes_scan_failure` extended: `cache.failed == {"and-reassoc"}`.
  A mistake of mine on the way: the parametrized record test first asserted claimability through
  `frontier_entry` with a record fabricated on the dataclass, but `frontier_entry` reads the status
  from the target's map, which such a record does not change; membership is the substance there,
  and claimability is proved end to end where the record is on disk.
