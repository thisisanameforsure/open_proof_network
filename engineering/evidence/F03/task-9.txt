F03-T9 · a hole waiting only for its witness is on the frontier, and claimable (R5, Q11) · 2026-09-16

THE FINDING, LIVE
-----------------
An agent testing the network submitted a partial on erdos-412; graph PR #71 merged at 09:10:26,
step 9 skipped because the target's registry evidence waives it (F14-T5). The post-merge job then
did exactly what D-29 asks: it created the hole

  erdos-412--h1   origin: skeleton-hole   status: blocked   cause: witness-missing

with a generated statement (a descent lemma over (σ 1) iterates) and a witness *slot* —
`theorem witness : True := by sorry` — and derived the root `blocked` on it.

The target then left the frontier entirely: 23 entries to 22, with no erdos-412 entry at all,
while targets/index.json went on publishing `claimable: true, not_claimable: []`. So the Targets
page invited a contributor to a target whose frontier offered nothing to work on, and
`list_frontier` — what an agent actually reads — showed the conjecture as gone.

WHY IT HAPPENED
---------------
products.in_frontier admitted `status in FRONTIER_STATUSES` ("ready", "speculative") plus open
variants. A hole is `blocked` from the moment the job writes it, so *no hole created since F07/F11
gave that job a caller had ever reached the frontier* — the tutorial's two have been invisible
since 2026-09-13, which the live contribution tester reported as "they are off the frontier" and
only half of which was fixed (the propose_witness tool landed; the visibility did not).

That is a defect against D-29, not a design choice. The decisions document says it four times:
"partial holes enter the frontier as compiler-derived children on gate pass"; "Residual holes
enter the frontier directly as compiler-derived children (D-29) once they pass the mechanical
gate; no human promotion step"; "k holes enter the frontier as compiler-derived children (D-29);
root stays open; its holes are new attack-route nodes"; "the holes enter the frontier as children
under D-29 once they pass the statement-side gate". D-25 publishes `origin: skeleton-hole` as a
frontier field to filter on, which is dead weight if no hole is ever an entry. So no decisions
amendment was needed; R5's enumeration was the stale text and it is corrected.

THE CHANGE
----------
gate/opn_gate/graph.py      awaiting_witness(node, status_of) — blocked *only* by an unfilled
                            witness slot, built on blocked_because so the distinction is drawn
                            once: a node waiting on an unproved dependency is not this.
gate/opn_gate/products.py   in_frontier gains status_of and admits such a hole; frontier_entry's
                            claimable expression gains the same case; status_or_ready +
                            functools.partial so the predicate is bound per target.
engineering/specs/features/F03.html   R5's membership clause, F03-Q11, the T9 ledger line.

Claimable as well as listed (the owner's call, 2026-09-16): Q8's premise — "a claim on it could
not be worked" — is false here. The witness *is* the work, and POST /proposals/witness accepts
exactly a hole whose cause is witness-missing (hole_awaiting_witness) and refuses a witness
carrying a sorry. The variant rule is untouched, and a dependency-blocked node stays off.
