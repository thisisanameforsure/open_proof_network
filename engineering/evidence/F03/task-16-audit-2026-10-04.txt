F03-T16 (audit 2026-10-04): frontier/v4 — each entry says its node's status, cause and what it needs
=====================================================================================================

Finding: frontier/v3 entries carried no status, cause or next action. Live, three holes waiting
only for a witness (blocked, cause witness-missing) were published claimable: true; an agent that
claimed one was then answered 409 node-blocked. Owner approved the schema bump (sign-off S4).

Change
- gate/schemas/frontier/v4.json (new; HASHES gains one appended line, D-34): v3 plus three
  required entry fields: status (graph/v4's status enum), cause (null or graph.json's cause
  pattern), needs (null | proof | witness | dependencies).
- needs is derived from the facts products.in_frontier already reads (products.needs_of):
    proof         status ready or speculative (graph.FRONTIER_STATUSES)
    witness       blocked, and graph.awaiting_witness (cause witness-missing)
    dependencies  blocked on unproved dependencies, cause null (an open variant, R5)
    null          anything else on the frontier: blocked under a refuted dep (cause dep-refuted,
                  a curator acts), a curator's stale/disputed record on a variant; also
                  superseded or circular (never listed).
  No other value is derivable from graph.py/products.py.
- claimable = target claimable AND needs == "proof". A witness-only hole stays on the frontier
  (D-29, Q11) and reads claimable: false, needs: witness.
- cause is graph.cause_of, extracted from graph.derive_causes so graph.json and the frontier read
  one function.
