Ledger line (F13 §10 Tasks): T29 ✓ (audit 2026-10-04): opn_gate.codes, 348 codes with source,
step, meaning and remedy; held to the source by an AST walk (test_error_codes.py).

Q entry (F13-Q?): "The catalog's source field has a third value, both." The task named
gate|api. 26 codes are emitted by the gate and refused early by the service under the same name
(the F13-T21 pre-flights); 'both' says the remedy is the same wherever it is met, and the test
derives the value from where the walk finds the code, so it cannot drift. MCP adapter errors
(arguments-invalid, tool-unknown, filter-unknown, unparseable, context-*, service-error) are
catalogued as api: the adapter is part of the service package, and an agent meets them in the
same 'error' field. step is the number the verdict carries: admission numbers its checks after
D-4's (the relation check is 9), so relation-* say 9 although D-4's step 9 is review. Pass
records that a verdict carries as a code (alternate-submission, artifact-proof, hazards-
acknowledged, partial-submission ...) have rows too, with "Not an error" as their meaning and
"Nothing to do" as their remedy, because they arrive in the same diagnostic field.

Follow-up worth having: the walk shows the gate emits 'failed' ("check failed without detail")
and 'unexpected-error'; both are catalogued as "report it", which is honest, but they are the two
codes a contributor can do nothing with.
