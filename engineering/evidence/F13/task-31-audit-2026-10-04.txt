== Proposed Q entry (F13-Q32)
F13-Q32 (T31). The exhibit pre-flight's node modules are the gate's: checks.EXHIBIT_NODE_MODULES is
exhibits.EXHIBIT_NODE_MODULES, which says what exhibits.stage_node compiles (the node's Context)
and what compiled_exhibit admits for a circularity claim (the two nodes' Contexts). Before, the
service let an exhibit import its node's Statement and answered `elaborates`, while the gate,
which copies the Statement but never compiles it for an exhibit, refused it
`exhibit-elaboration`. Such an exhibit is now not sent (`skipped`, as one naming another node's
module was already), so no hosted check is spent and no receipt vouches for it. Not done here,
and the owner's to decide: refusing it 422 before the append opens, since the gate's refusal is
certain without Lean (the principle of 2026-09-24); and whether the gate should compile the
Statement for an exhibit instead, which would widen what an exhibit may import (D-16) rather than
narrow the pre-flight.
