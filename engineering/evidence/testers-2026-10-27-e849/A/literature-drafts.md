# Literature record drafts for the new holes (for whoever files them; node ids once the holes exist)

All three: status `known` (published, not formalised). Source: K. Matomäki, M. Radziwiłł, X. Shao, T. Tao, J. Teräväinen,
"Singmaster's conjecture in the interior of Pascal's triangle", Quart. J. Math. 73 (2022) 1137–1177, arXiv:2106.03335,
https://doi.org/10.1093/qmath/haac006.

- h_dist (--h3--h1): "Section 1.2 (strategy of proof): Proposition 1.9 (distance estimate) with the size bounds (1.5), (1.7)
  gives, for two solutions in the region exp(log^{2/3+ε} n) ≤ m ≤ n/2 and t large, m − m', n' − n ≪ exp(O((log log t)^{1−ε'}))
  for every ε' < ε/(2/3+ε). The node is this with ε = 1/6, ε' = 3/20, constants absorbed into a₁ (exponent 9/10 > 17/20)."
- h_kane (--h3--h2): "Proposition 1.10 (Kane-type estimate), proved in Section 2 from derivative estimates (Prop 2.1, Cor 2.3):
  for a left-half solution (n,m), at most one other (n',m') with m' < m, n' > n and |m−m'|+|n−n'| ≪ exp((log log t)^{1−ε}),
  t large. The node is this with ε = 1/20: c·exp(x^{19/20}) ≥ exp(x^{9/10}) for x large, whatever c. Rows ≤ solutions."
- h_prop19 (--h3--h1--h1, third level): "Proposition 1.9 (distance estimate) with ε = 1/20 (exponent 2/3 + 1/20 = 43/60),
  proved in Section 3 from the equidistribution estimate Proposition 1.12 (Section 4). The node writes ≪_ε as an explicit
  C ≥ 0 and 'sufficiently large' as a₁; the signed differences of the paper are stated as absolute values (symmetric by
  swapping the roles of the two solutions)."
