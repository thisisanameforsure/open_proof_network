# Literature drafts for the nine per-pair holes of --h2--h1--h1 (B/D to file once they exist; all status `known`)
Index reference for every one: A. Blokhuis, A. E. Brouwer, B. M. M. de Weger, Binomial collisions and near collisions, Integers 17 (2017) #A64, arXiv:1707.06893, Theorem 2.2 (first case) and its proof, which attributes each pair.
- h_23 (2,3): È. T. Avanesov, Solution of a problem on figurate numbers, Acta Arith. 12 (1966/67) 409–420. Half-triangle solutions (n,m) = (16,10), (56,22), (120,36). Internal theorem number not verified (paper not reachable).
- h_24 (2,4): Á. Pintér, Publ. Math. Debrecen 47 (1995) 411–415; B. M. M. de Weger, A binomial Diophantine equation, Quart. J. Math. Oxford (2) 47 (1996) 221–231. Only (21,10). Theorem numbers not verified.
- h_25 (2,5): Y. Bugeaud, M. Mignotte, S. Siksek, M. Stoll, Sz. Tengely, Integral points on hyperelliptic curves, Algebra & Number Theory 2 (2008) 859–885, arXiv:0801.4459, **Theorem 2** (verified from the arXiv text): all integer solutions of C(Y,2) = C(X,5) are X ≤ 7 trivial, (X,Y) = (15,78), (19,153) and their mirrors Y ↦ 1−Y. Half triangle: (78,15), (153,19).
- h_26, h_28, h_36, h_46, h_48: R. J. Stroeker, B. M. M. de Weger, Elliptic binomial Diophantine equations, Math. Comp. 68 (1999) 1257–1281 (AMS page answered 403 from here; theorem numbers not verified). Half-triangle solutions: (2,6): (78,14); (2,8): (221,17); (3,6), (4,6), (4,8): none.
- h_34 (3,4): L. J. Mordell, On the integer solutions of y(y+1) = x(x+1)(x+2), Pacific J. Math. 13 (1963) 1347–1351, via the reduction x = n−2, y = m(m−3)/2 (C(n,3) = C(m,4) ⇔ y(y+1) = x(x+1)(x+2)); the nonneg solutions (0,0), (1,2), (5,14) give n ∈ {2,3,7}, m ∈ {3,4,7}, all outside 6 ≤ n, 8 ≤ m. Attribution as in BBdW's proof of Thm 2.2.
