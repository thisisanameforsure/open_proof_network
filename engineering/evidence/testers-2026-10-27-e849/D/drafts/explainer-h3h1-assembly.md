## Overview

The statement (hole `h_dist` of the partial on --h3) is the distance bound for two interior solutions: for $a$ large, two solutions $(n, k)$, $(n', k')$ of $\binom{n}{k} = a$ with $e^{(\log n)^{5/6}} \le k \le n/2$ and likewise for $(n', k')$ satisfy $|n - n'| + |k - k'| \le e^{(\log\log a)^{9/10}}$. The assembly is the deduction of (1.10) in Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Shao–Tao–Teräväinen §1.2 made explicit: the distance estimate (their Proposition 1.9) is one hole, the size bookkeeping (1.5), (1.7) a second, and a real-analysis lemma absorbing the implied constant the third; everything else is proved here. Write $X := \log\log a$ throughout.

## The three holes and the threshold {steps: hall s2 s3}

`hall` states the three holes as one conjunction: `h_prop19`, Proposition 1.9 with $\varepsilon = 1/20$ — a constant $C \ge 0$ and a threshold $a_1$ such that two half-triangle solutions have $|k - k'| \le C e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$, and, when both columns are at least $e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$, also $|n - n'| \le C e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$; `h_size`, the size bounds for a half-triangle solution ($\log a \le k \log n$; $k \le 2\log a$ when $k \ge 3$; and, in the interior, $k \ge 3$ and $\log n \le (\log k)^{6/5}$); and `h_absorb`, for every constant $C'$ a threshold $X_0$ beyond which $(1 + (X+1)^{6/5})^{43/60} \le X - \tfrac{6}{5}\log(X+1)$ and $C' e^{(1 + (X+1)^{6/5})^{43/60}} \le e^{X^{9/10}}$. The conjunction is taken apart, `h_absorb` is applied with the constant $2C$ to obtain $X_0$, and the threshold for $a$ is chosen as $a_0 := \max\bigl(a_1,\ \lceil e^{e^{\max(X_0, 0)}} \rceil + 2\bigr)$, so that $a \ge a_1$ and $\log\log a \ge \max(X_0, 0)$ for every $a \ge a_0$.

## Unpacking the hypotheses {steps: ha2 hac haR ha_pos hloga hloga_pos hX0 hXX hXnn s13 s14 s15 s16 s17 s18 s19 hkle hkle' hlog2}

For $a \ge a_0$ the two parts of the threshold are recorded (`ha₂`, `hac`), and from the second $e^{e^{\max(X_0,0)}} \le a$ as reals (`haR`), hence $a > 0$, $e^{\max(X_0,0)} \le \log a$, $\log a > 0$, and with $X := \log\log a$: $\max(X_0, 0) \le X$, so $X_0 \le X$ and $X \ge 0$. Applying `h_absorb` at $X$ gives the two absorption inequalities. The two interior solutions are taken apart into their four conditions each, `h_size` is applied to both, the interior condition yields $k, k' \ge 3$ and $\log n \le (\log k)^{6/5}$, $\log n' \le (\log k')^{6/5}$, and then $k, k' \le 2\log a$. Finally $\log 2 < 1$ is recorded from Mathlib's decimal bound.

## Bounding the rows and the columns by $X$ {steps: gen s24 s25 hnpos hnpos' hlogsum hlogsum_nn}

The lemma `gen`, for any half-triangle solution $(m, j)$ with $3 \le j \le 2\log a$, $\log a \le j\log m$ and $\log m \le (\log j)^{6/5}$, gives $\log m \le (X+1)^{6/5}$ and $X - \tfrac{6}{5}\log(X+1) \le \log j$: from $j \le 2\log a$, $\log j \le \log 2 + X \le X + 1$, so $\log m \le (\log j)^{6/5} \le (X+1)^{6/5}$; and from $\log a \le j\log m$, $\log j \ge \log(\log a / \log m) = X - \log\log m \ge X - \tfrac{6}{5}\log\log j \ge X - \tfrac{6}{5}\log(X+1)$. It is applied to $(n, k)$ and to $(n', k')$. Then $n, n' \le e^{(X+1)^{6/5}}$, so $n + n' \le 2e^{(X+1)^{6/5}}$ and $\log(n + n') \le \log 2 + (X+1)^{6/5} \le L := 1 + (X+1)^{6/5}$ (`hlogsum`), with $L \ge 0$.

## Applying the distance estimate {steps: hE hEk hEk' s33 hdn' s35 s36 s37 s38}

Let $E := e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$, the radius of Proposition 1.9. Monotonicity gives $E \le e^{L^{43/60}}$ (`hE`), and the first absorption inequality with the lower bound on $\log k$ gives $e^{L^{43/60}} \le e^{X - \frac{6}{5}\log(X+1)} \le e^{\log k} = k$, so $E \le k$, and likewise $E \le k'$ (`hEk`, `hEk'`): both columns are at least the radius, which is the hypothesis of the second half of `h_prop19`. Applying `h_prop19` to the two solutions gives $|k - k'| \le CE$ and, since $E \le k, k'$, $|n - n'| \le CE$. Adding, $|n - n'| + |k - k'| \le 2CE \le 2C e^{L^{43/60}} \le e^{X^{9/10}}$, the last step being the second absorption inequality with the constant $2C$; and $e^{X^{9/10}} = e^{(\log\log a)^{9/10}}$ is the bound claimed.
