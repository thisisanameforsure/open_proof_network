----------

in the graph's own defs/: Divides, IsPrime, fact. Five nodes: fact-pos, dvd-fact, prime-divisor,

API applies to it directly — and it yields a three-node graph, the bottom of R7's band, too thin
for the run to exercise parallel claiming or a variant proposal.

a local copy transfers core lemmas over an rfl-shaped bridge, and well-founded recursion puts
`decreasing_by` plumbing in front of every prover before any mathematics starts.
