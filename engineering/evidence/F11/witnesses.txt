WHAT A WITNESS HAS TO BE
------------------------
Step 7's expected type is the statement's hypotheses closed over the variables they mention
(F01-R3): for `theorem s : ∀ (x₁ : α₁) … (hₖ : Pₖ), C` it is `∃ x₁ …, P₁ ∧ … ∧ Pₖ`. A hole
inherits the holes before it as hypotheses (F11-Q22), so h3 and h4 can only be witnessed by
*proving* the earlier lemmas — the witness for a later hole is real mathematics, not a token.
The four expected types, as the metaprogram derives them:

  h1  ∃ n k, 0 < k ∧ k ≤ n
  h2  ∃ n m, (∀ k, 0 < k → k ≤ n → Opn.Divides k n.factorial) ∧ 2 ≤ m
  h3  ∃ n d m, (h1's lemma at n) ∧ (∀ m, 2 ≤ m → ∃ p, Opn.IsPrime p ∧ Opn.Divides p m)
                ∧ Opn.Divides d m ∧ Opn.Divides d (m + 1)
  h4  ∃ n k, (h1's lemma at n) ∧ (h2's lemma) ∧ (h3's lemma)

h1 and h2 are discharged by choice: `n = k = 1` for the first, and `n = 0` for the second, which
makes `∀ k, 0 < k → k ≤ 0 → …` vacuous. h3 and h4 need two real lemmas, and both are in the
files that carry them:

  exists_prime_divisor  every m ≥ 2 has a prime divisor, by strong induction, with the
                        non-trivial divisor of a composite extracted classically
  dvd_consecutive       nothing but 1 divides two consecutive numbers

Both are written over the graph's own `Opn.Divides` and `Opn.IsPrime` (D-6: Mathlib's `Dvd` API
does not apply to them by name), and `Opn.Divides a b` is core `a ∣ b` written out, so the core
divisibility lemmas apply to it definitionally — which is the bridge F11-T1 said would be a
lemma rather than a citation.

LEAN CORE ONLY, AND WHY
-----------------------
No Mathlib name appears in any of the four, although the statement's environment offers Mathlib
(the witness is elaborated on top of the statement's environment, which is why a hole's
Witness.lean carries no imports of its own). Two reasons: a core proof can be checked on a laptop
with no Mathlib checkout — this one has 6.6 GiB free and a Mathlib checkout is 6.8 GiB — and it
cannot break when a Mathlib lemma is renamed under the pin.

The finding is not in the witness. Step 6 runs the checkers over `Statement.lean` and nothing
else (`opn_gate/steps/hazards.py` builds its request from `node_dir / "Statement.lean"`), so a

    $ grep -l acknowledged_hazards targets/euclid-primes/nodes/*/META.yaml

So h2, h3 and h4 carry an unacknowledged hazard in statements the post-merge job generated from
the assembly, and their META.yaml has no `acknowledged_hazards` because nothing wrote one. Step 6
refuses them, and `POST /proposals/witness` pushes only `Witness.lean` (F08-R5), so no proposal
can ever supply the acknowledgment. Those three nodes cannot accept any submission from anyone
until a curator writes the acknowledgment into their META.yaml. h1 merged only because its
obligation happens to contain no literal comparison.

Logged as F07-Q19. The three pull requests are left open: they are correct work waiting on the
acknowledgment, and they become mergeable the moment it lands.
