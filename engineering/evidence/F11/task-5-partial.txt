ONE DESIGN NOTE WORTH REVIEWING
-------------------------------
`import-fc` derives only the upstream half of the record — track, provenance, sources — and takes
the rest from a `--from <target.yaml>` the curator writes. D-6's five artifacts are not waived by
an import: prior art, domains and the paraphrase are exactly what a person has to supply, and
deriving them would be inventing them. So an import is intake with the provenance filled in, not
a second intake path, and every refusal in `intake new` still applies to it.
