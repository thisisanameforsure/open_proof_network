WHAT A MATHLIB PIN HAD TO TRAVEL THROUGH (three gaps, not one)
-------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Step 1 refused every Mathlib-pinned graph outright ("mathlib-unsupported").
     Now: ToolchainStep hands mathlib_sha to the seam; LocalToolchain.resolve finds the checkout
     at OPN_MATHLIB_HOME/<sha> (stamp, lean-toolchain and oleans all checked) and the resolved
     pin carries library_path; ResolvedToolchain.search_path is the ONE place LEAN_PATH's order is
     decided — build dir, Mathlib and its packages, the toolchain's lib — for lean, leanchecker,
     the axiom probe and every metaprogram. The sandbox runs resolve inside the container, so
     the image's path and a laptop's path both just work (Q14).
  2. Nothing staged targets/<id>/defs/ — a node importing Defs.X failed step 4 with an unknown
     module. Now: opn_gate.defs (order by imports, four refusals by name, compile-before-Context)
     called from every compiler of a Context: replay, admission's statement check, the tag scan,
     exhibits, the consolidation probe, intake's definition check, the cache build (Q13).
  3. products refused a Mathlib graph ("need the sandboxed scan"). Now: the scan builds the
     sandbox from the spec whenever a target pins Mathlib, never otherwise (Q15).

Plus the image itself (gate/Dockerfile MATHLIB_SHA + install-mathlib.sh + the stamp), the tag
(image_tag/build_image/ensure_image with the sha), the publish matrix over gate/mathlib-pins.txt
with the Mathlib label pin_image.py --verify reads back, post-create proving the checkout is in
the image, and OPN_MATHLIB_HOME in config.

TWO THINGS THE FIXTURE FOUND
----------------------------
  - A proof cannot add an import: proof-is-statement (F00-R19) compares headers, so the first
    fixture failed step 2 with "diverges at line 2: import Mathlib.Tactic.Linarith". The
    statement carries the imports its provers may use (Q16); T4 seeds the on-ramp statements so.
  - Mathlib's manifest lists `Cli` (its cache tool's dependency) with no oleans; requiring a lib
    dir per package refused a real checkout. Packages without oleans are skipped (Q14).
