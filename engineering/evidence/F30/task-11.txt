== 1. Design ==

- `dag.proposed(nodes)`: the band's cruxes (proposed_for names a row, no relation, not a dep of
  any row). `dag.band_of(nodes)`: those plus their hole children (hole origin, id under
  `<crux>--h`), transitively. `pointers` and `edge proposed` go.
- The band is its own `<svg class="dag band">` under the main drawing (heading "Proposed
  statements" + one line of help), laid out by `dag.layout(..., apart=False)` seeded like every
  view, so a lone crux is a row, never a "no line joins" group. Not in the layouts JSON; the
  script never moves it.
- Cross-region lines (`use`, `lemma`, a dep) are `path.band-link` in an overlay svg with no `d`
  at rest; the script draws each from the pills' boxes while lit (hover previews, click pins,
  hash `band=<id>`), and lights the band's own lines through junctions as F30-T6 does.
- `routes_view.drawn` visits the band's cruxes too (sink `band`), so their routes have junctions;
  `merged_nodes` puts the drawn variants on the root row as `relations`, which `dag.lines` draws
  as `edge relation <label>` and the main svg colours.
