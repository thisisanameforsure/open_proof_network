That annex announced a three-hole skeleton (`residue`, `denominator_integral`, `numerator_integral`). The partial proof actually submitted for this node (pull request #290, precheck verdict pass, job 01M3QC1M98WHBP84NNERK11NCB) has one hole, `numerator_integral`; the other two steps are proved inside it:

1. Partial fractions. For n >= 1 and m >= 1, the E(n) summand at x = 2^m equals sum_k A(n,k)/(1 - c 2^k x) + sum_{i=1..n-1} B(n,i) x^(-i). Proved from Mathlib's Lagrange interpolation (`Lagrange.eq_interpolate`, `Lagrange.eval_interpolate_not_at_node`) applied to Q(x) = -prod_{t=1..n-1}(x - 2^t) at the nodes 1/(c 2^k), plus a geometric-sum induction.
2. Residue identity. E(n) = beta0(n) z - alpha0(n), by summing that identity over m >= 1 (the first n - 1 summands of E vanish), with geometric series and the tail of z.
3. Denominator integrality. 9^n W(n) beta0(n) = 3 (n-2)! prod_{k=1..n}(3 - 8 2^k) prod_{k=(n+1)/2..n}(1 - 2^k) * (3^(n-1) beta0(n)), and 3^(n-1) beta0(n) is the Y^(n-1) coefficient of the integer polynomial -prod_{t<n}(3Y - 2^t) * prod_{j=1..n} sum_{i<n} (8 2^j Y)^i. The proof takes the polynomial identity -prod_{t<n}(3Y - 2^t) = sum_k C_k prod_{j != k}(1 - 8 2^j Y) (agreement at n points) and uses (1 - rY) sum_{i<n}(rY)^i = 1 - (rY)^n to read the coefficient modulo Y^n. No q-binomial coefficients are needed.

What remains, `numerator_integral`: 9^n W(n) alpha0(n) is an integer for n >= 1. Numerically (Python, exact rationals; not a Lean check) alpha0 = S1 - S2 with S1 = sum_k A(n,k) sum_{i<=k} (1 - c 2^i)^(-1) and S2 = sum_{i=1..n-1} B(n,i)/(2^i - 1), and 9^n W(n) S1 and 9^n W(n) S2 are each integers for n = 1..25; neither single terms 9^n W(n) A(n,k) nor 3^(n-1) times the tail sums of A(n,k) are integers in general. The 1/(2^i - 1) denominators are what the factors prod_{k=(n+1)/2..n}(1 - 2^k) and (n-2)! in W(n) are for; Trevor Morris's lean-gallery (Apache-2.0, commit efc72e14, LeanGallery/NumberTheory/Erdos1050/Lemma3.lean) proves the corresponding Lemma 3 by a 2-adic and an odd-denominator argument.
