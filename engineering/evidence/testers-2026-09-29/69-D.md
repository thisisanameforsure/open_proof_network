## Mathematics

What I established (every claim marked with how it was checked):

- **Integrality for any denominator** (`69-D/tail_integral.lean`, checked by /check): if Σ_n ω(n)/2^n = a/b with b > 0, then b·T_N ∈ ℤ for every N, where T_N = Σ_{j≥0} ω(N+1+j)/2^(j+1). Generalises the dyadic lemma of annex b32cd0b1; per annex PR #256 the same fact is already inside the merged partial on erdos-69--h2-v2, so it is not new on the record and I did not submit it separately.
- **Tail recurrence** (`69-D/tail_step.lean`, checked by /check): T_N = ω(N+1)/2 + T_{N+1}/2 (given summability). Also on record as `tail_recurrence` in annex 25863f56.
- **ω on a product without coprimality** (`69-D/omega_mul_gcd.lean`, checked by /check): ω(a·n) + ω(gcd a n) = ω a + ω n for a, n ≠ 0.
- **Dilation identity, pointwise and for tails** (`69-D/dilated_tail.lean`, checked by /check): ω(a·n) = ω n + ω a − Σ_{p∈a.primeFactors}[p ∣ n], and Σ_k ω(a(m+k+1))/2^(k+1) = T_m + ω a − Σ_{p | a} Σ_k [p ∣ m+k+1]/2^(k+1). This is step 3(b) of the external proof's architecture as described in annex PR #256 (which gave the statement only). Submitted as annex PR #261 on erdos-69.
- **Closed form of the correction** (`69-D/indicator_closed.lean`, checked by /check): Σ_k [p ∣ m+k+1]/2^(k+1) = 2^(m mod p)/(2^p − 1) for p > 0. With the dilation identity this gives a complete proof (`69-D/9f8cb4ea_Proof.lean`, checked by /check, one theorem, no helper declarations) of 69-C's proposed `Opn.erdos_69_dilated_tail_closed` (spec-9f8cb4ea, PR #271). Not submitted: the node is not merged yet.
- **Why the 1948 method does not transfer** (informal, not checked): the contradiction on the record's chain needs ω(N+1+j) mod 2^(j+1) controlled on ~log log N consecutive integers (h4, which restates the root). For d, CRT gives such control since d is multiplicative (p^e ∥ n with 2^m | e+1 forces 2^m | d(n) whatever the cofactor); ω is additive, so the free cofactor shifts every residue, and a CRT modulus that also clears all primes up to y exceeds N once y ~ log N. The dilation identity swaps the cofactor's contribution for divisibility indicators that CRT controls exactly.
- **Literature** (read, not verified): formal-conjectures 257.lean cites Erdős 1948 (J. Indian Math. Soc. (N.S.), 63–66) for Σ d(n)/2^n only; 69.lean credits Tao with "69 is the prime case of 257" and gives no proof reference.
