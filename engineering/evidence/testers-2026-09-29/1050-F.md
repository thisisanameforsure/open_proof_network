## Bugs

### B1: `check_lean` with the `node_id` of a proposed (unmerged) node answers `409 node-pending`
- Severity: minor. Where: MCP `check_lean` (POST /check), modes `verify`/`check` with `node_id`.
- Sent (19:10:40Z): `{"target_id":"erdos-1050","node_id":"spec-1a5ab7c3","mode":"verify","content":<my proof>}` while PR #228 was gating.
- Got: isError, `{"status":409,"body":{"error":"node-pending","message":"spec-1a5ab7c3 is proposed in pull request #228, which is waiting on: gate; nothing can be prechecked or appended against it until that merges", ...}}`.
- Expected: the fast check is not a precheck and the proposed statement is on the branch (the service already serves it as `proposed_statement`), so verifying a proof against it would let a prover finish before the merge. The guide's wording ("nothing can be prechecked or appended") does not mention /check. Workaround: check without `node_id` (works, but no `verify` comparison against the node's header with the service-added `import Nodes.«id».Context`). Reproduced 1×.

### B2: no hazard checker flags truncating ℕ division by a literal (`n / 2`, `n * (n + 1) / 2`)
- Severity: minor (docs/design). Where: `check_lean` mode `hazards` and step 6; guide "The gate contract" item 6 ("integer truncation").
- Sent (19:12:40Z): hazards on a statement containing `Finset.Ioc (n / 2) n` and `2 ^ (n * (n + 1) / 2)` over ℕ.
- Got: no `int-trunc` and no `div-zero` finding for either. The inlined checker source says why: int-trunc covers ℤ only ("Division on ℕ is div-zero's business and is not flagged here"), and div-zero skips a syntactically non-zero divisor. So ℕ floor division by a literal is flagged by nothing.
- Expected: either a finding (it is the classic truncation hazard) or the guide saying "integer truncation" means ℤ only. In my statements the truncation is intended (⌊n/2⌋; n(n+1) is even), so nothing was lost here. Reproduced 2× (two statements).
