The literature route, what of it is on this record, and what a Lean proof still needs. (1) Identity: sum_n omega(n)/2^n = sum_p 1/(2^p - 1), by summing 2^(-pk) over prime p and k >= 1. Gate-checked here as erdos-69--h1-v2. formal-conjectures (FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/69.lean, variant specialisation_of_erdos_257) credits this observation to Tao: Erdos 69 is the case A = primes of Erdos 257, the irrationality of sum over n in A of 1/(2^n - 1) for infinite A, which 257.lean files as research open (target erdos-257 on this graph). (2) Provenance of the irrationality step, to be settled by a curator. 257.lean cites P. Erdos, On arithmetical properties of Lambert series, J. Indian Math. Soc. (N.S.) (1948), 63-66 (as 257.lean cites it) for the case A = all n, i.e. sum d(n)/2^n. I found no source on hand that credits the 1948 paper with the prime case, yet this target's prior_art summary ("solved ... by P. Erdos 1948, standing since 1948") and root annex 84315a59 ("Lemma 2 (Erdos 1948)") both do. The earlier approach record 20260923T145333Z quotes erdosproblems.com/69 as crediting Tao-Teravainen 2025; erdosproblems.com was unreachable from my container, so neither attribution is checked here. (3) Reduction (on the record): if the sum is a/b then M_N = b*T_N, with T_N = sum_{j>=0} omega(N+1+j)/2^(j+1), is an integer for every N (general b: checked by /check, tail_integral in my evidence; dyadic b: annex b32cd0b1), M_{N+1} = 2*M_N - b*omega(N+1) (checked by /check), and 1 <= M_N <= b*(log2(N+1)+1) (holes h2, h3 of erdos-69--h2-v2--h1-v2, gate-checked). Iterating k steps, b*A(N,k) = -M_{N+k} mod 2^k with A(N,k) = sum_{j<k} omega(N+1+j)*2^(k-1-j), so a contradiction needs one window of k ~ log2(b*log2 N) consecutive integers on which b*A(N,k) mod 2^k falls outside the top block of about b*log2 N residues. That is hole h4 of erdos-69--h2-v2--h1-v2, equivalent to the root (merged circularity claims). (4) Why the 1948 divisor argument does not transfer (informal, not checked): the residue of A(N,k) mod 2^k depends on omega(N+1+j) mod 2^(j+1). d is multiplicative, so a congruence on d(n) can be forced by one exact prime-power divisor whatever the cofactor: if p^e exactly divides n and 2^m divides e+1, then 2^m divides d(n). omega is additive, omega(n) = omega(forced part) + omega(cofactor), so the free cofactor that the Chinese remainder theorem leaves shifts every residue; bounding it two-sidedly on about log log N consecutive integers at once is sieve or correlation territory, since a CRT modulus that also removes all primes up to y exceeds N once y is of order log N. Prescribing omega mod 2 on arbitrarily long windows would be a Chowla-type sign-pattern statement for (-1)^omega, which I believe is open, so the literature proof must use a weaker averaged input that I have not been able to read. A lemma naming that input without restating the root would be a real crux node; h4 and everything above it restate the root.
