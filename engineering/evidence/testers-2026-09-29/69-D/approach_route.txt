Target-level summary of the literature route and of what a Lean proof on this network still needs. Sources and the external proof's architecture are in the annex on erdos-69 opened as graph PR #256 by t0929-3 (open when this was filed); I re-checked only what is marked. (1) Attribution. formal-conjectures' 257.lean cites P. Erdos, On arithmetical properties of Lambert series, J. Indian Math. Soc. (N.S.) (1948), 63-66, for the divisor case sum d(n)/2^n (variant erdos_257.variants.tsum_top), and 69.lean credits Tao with the remark that 69 is the prime case of 257 (checked by reading both files). The PR #256 annex credits the omega case to T. Tao and J. Teravainen, arXiv:2512.01739 (2025), unconditionally, and K. Pratt, arXiv:2409.15185, conditionally on prime k-tuples (not re-checked: arXiv and erdosproblems.com are unreachable from my container). So this target's prior_art line ("solved ... by P. Erdos 1948") and root annex 84315a59 ("Lemma 2 (Erdos 1948)") attribute the omega case to the 1948 paper; a curator should correct target.yaml, which no submission may touch. (2) Why the chain on this record stops at h4. If the sum is a/b, then M_N = b*T_N with T_N = sum_{j>=0} omega(N+1+j)/2^(j+1) is an integer for every N and every b (checked by /check: tail_integral), M_{N+1} = 2*M_N - b*omega(N+1) (checked by /check: tail_step), and 1 <= M_N <= b*(log2(N+1)+1) (holes h2, h3 of erdos-69--h2-v2--h1-v2, gate-checked). A contradiction then needs omega(N+1+j) mod 2^(j+1) controlled on one window of about log2(b*log2 N) consecutive integers, which is h4, equivalent to the root. The 1948 CRT method gives that control for d, because d is multiplicative: if p^e exactly divides n and 2^m divides e+1, then 2^m divides d(n) whatever the cofactor. omega is additive, so the prime count of the free cofactor shifts every residue, and CRT cannot remove it: a modulus that also clears every prime up to y exceeds N once y is about log N (informal, not checked). (3) What removes the cofactor. For nonzero a and n, omega(a*n) + omega(gcd a n) = omega a + omega n (proved; checked by /check: omega_mul_add_omega_gcd). So a dilated tail sum_k omega(a*(m+k+1))/2^(k+1) equals T_m + omega(a) minus terms that depend only on which primes of a divide m+k+1, a condition CRT controls exactly; signed combinations of dilated tails are how the external proof described in the PR #256 annex avoids prime-tuple inputs. (4) Lean. A complete proof exists outside the network: github.com/plby/lean-proofs, src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos69.lean declares theorem erdos_69 (I checked that the file and theorem exist; I did not build it). The repository has no LICENSE file at its root, and it is written for Lean v4.33.0 while this target pins v4.33.1. It cannot be copied here until its licence is settled, and its hard step needs about fifteen definitions (patterns, dilations, the progression, a characteristic function) that a hole cannot introduce and a contributor cannot add to defs/. The route on this network is therefore: a curator settles the licence and imports those definitions into targets/erdos-69/defs/; then the dilation identity, the integrality lemma, the rational-side limit and the unconditional characteristic-function limit (the one step irrationality does not imply) become ordinary nodes, and the root closes from them. Until then every hole phrased through the tails restates the root, as the merged circularity claims on erdos-69--h2-v2, erdos-69--h2-v2--h1-v2 and erdos-69--h2-v2--h1-v2--h4 show.
