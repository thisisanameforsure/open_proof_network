### What now works that did not this morning

A pull request no longer has to be a proof. The gate classifies one into `proof`, `partial`,
`append` or `explainer` from its paths alone, and each mode has its own checks: an append merges
on its schema, an explainer needs a merged proof and is refused as a misfiled annex without one,
a partial has its holes extracted and D-12's offload rule applied to them, and a counterexample
or vacuity certificate is checked against the type D-12 says it must declare.

The service can open all of it. `POST /submissions` binds a submission to a passing precheck by
the same identity over the same bytes, then opens a pull request authored and signed off by the
ledger identity with the App as committer. `POST /postmortems`, `/annexes` and
`/approach-records` do the same for the records that claim nothing.

And a node can enter the graph without anyone approving it: `opn-gate admit` runs the eight
checks D-29 needs to be mechanical, including D-30's relation proof, whose *direction* is
checked rather than believed.

## 6. Three things the real toolchain said that guessing would have got wrong

All three cost a real-tier run to find, and all three are in the evidence files:

1. **`have h : T := sorry` is an `Expr.letE`** in Lean 4.33 — not a beta-redex, not `letFun`.
   The hole extractor was written after printing the term rather than before.

2. **A theorem's proof term needs `value? (allowOpaque := true)`.** Without it the value is
   `none`, `value!` panics, and hole extraction silently reports *no holes* — the one failure a
   partial proof must never have. Caught by driving a two-hole fixture and seeing zero.

3. **Imports happen once per process.** A second `processHeader` returns an environment with no
   parser extensions, so the second file elaborated loses `∧`, `¬` and every other notation and
   fails with "expected token". Both metaprograms now synthesise one header from the union of
   their files' imports and import that once. The relation check additionally has to compile the
   *root's* `Context.lean`, which nothing else had reason to.
