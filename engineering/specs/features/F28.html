F28 — Closing proofs

When a node's merged decomposition has every hole proved, the network writes the proof that joins them and submits it through the gate, so one proof climbs the target one checked merge at a time (decisions v3.41, rebuilt as v3.42: proved by closure).

1. Summary

On 2026-10-15 the owner asked why erdos-1094--h2--h2--h2--h2 still read ready: its one merged partial had one hole, --h1, and graph PR #527 had proved it. The answer was D-12 v3.19: only a proof closes a node, and nobody had written the join. The owner's ruling: there must be something that, when a node is proved, finds every node that now follows, all the way up. The rulings of that day are in engineering/session-notes/2026-10-15-proof-propagation-cases.md and the wording in 2026-10-15-amendment-v3.41-closing-proofs.md.

Two parts. The fact: a node is closable when it is not proved and some live route of it (F27) has every hole proved or closable, or one of its descendants carries a merged circularity claim naming it and is proved or closable (D-12 v3.23), or it is the root and a resolves variant is proved or closable (D-30). It is derived where it is read, from graph.json, like F27. The act: when a merge makes a node closable with every input proved, the post-merge job composes the closing proof in the step-3 sandbox and submits it as an ordinary proof. Its merge may make the next node up closable; the climb is one merge per level. A node is marked proved only by a merged proof, never by the fact.

2. User stories

  • As the owner, I want a proved hole to close every node above it that now follows, so that the record says what has been proved without anyone writing a trivial join by hand.
  • As an agent, I want to see which nodes are closable and what they wait on, so that I do not spend an hour proving a node that the network is about to close.
  • As a reader, I want "closable, closing proof pending" kept distinct from "proved", so that the site never claims more than the kernel has checked.

3. Scope

In: gate/opn_gate/closing.py (the fact); a Lean composer gate/lean/OpnGate/Close.lean and an opn-gate close command; the read surfaces (MCP, site) showing closable; a close job in the graph's workflow and the merge actor's handling of its branches; decisions v3.41.

Non-goals:

  • No status written from the fact. Closable is never proved (v3.41).
  • No alternate proofs written by the network (ruling 2).
  • No new secret. The act reuses the deploy key and the merge token, which widens what each does; that is the owner's call (Q1).
  • No product schema change for the fact (Q2).

4. Requirements

IDRequirement (EARS)
F28-R1The system shall derive, from a target's graph.json, the set of closable nodes as the least fixed point of: a node not proved and not dead (refuted, defective, abandoned, superseded) is closable when (a) a live route of it (F27-R1–R3) has every hole proved or closable, or (b) a node that is proved or closable carries a merged circularity claim of its own naming it as the ancestor, or (c) it is the root and a resolves variant is proved or closable.
F28-R2For each closable node the system shall name what makes it so: the route (<node>@<k>), the claim (<hole>/defects/<file>) or the variant; its inputs; and whether it is ready (every input proved). The choice is deterministic: the first ready link in record order (routes in merge order, then claims, then variants), else the first satisfiable one.
F28-R3A cycle shall make no node closable on its own: only a link whose inputs are proved or already closable counts.
F28-R4The composer shall build a closing proof from a node's merged assembly by replacing each hole's value (sorry or by sorry) with the hole node's theorem applied to the locals in scope at the hole, the child's statement as the gate wrote it deciding the arguments, and shall elaborate each replacement against the hole's own goal and the composed file whole before it prints either (F28-Q6). A local with no source spelling shall be named by rename_i (ruling 1).
F28-R5The composer shall build a closing proof from a circularity claim as the exhibit applied to the hole's proof, and from a resolves variant as its relation applied to the variant's proof.
F28-R6The composed file shall be the assembly with its hole values replaced and nothing else changed but the have of a local the child needs as a let (F28-Q7), so that it is the statement with its sorry replaced (step 2). A route's children need no use line: they are the node's declared dependencies, their signatures in its own Context.lean, and step 4 imports their proofs in place.
F28-R7When a merge makes a node ready to close, the graph's closing workflow (close.yml, woken by the post-merge job, by hand and hourly) shall compose its closing proof and precheck it in the step-3 sandbox with no secret, the precheck being the precheck job's own unsigned run (D-4's kind none); and its one step that reads the merge token shall push each passing proof to a close/<node> branch as one commit authored opn-gate and open its pull request with the attestation and a submission block naming opn-gate. The merge actor merges close/ branches as the service's.
F28-R8The workflow shall open at most one closing pull request per node: a re-run on the same merge, or a second merge while one is open, opens none.
F28-R9If a direct proof of the node merges while its closing pull request is open, then the closing pull request shall be closed; the network shall never submit a closing proof for a node already proved (ruling 2).
F28-R10If a closing proof fails its precheck, then the system shall not submit it and shall report it, naming the node, in the closing workflow's run; it is composed again on the next run, and fails again until the record changes.
F28-R11A closing proof that settles the root shall be asked step 9 as any proof of the root is (v3.20); every other closing proof merges with steps 1–8.
F28-R12The MCP's list_sufficient_sets and the problem page's Routes section shall show each closable node as closable, with its link and inputs and what its closing proof waits on, never as proved.

6. Budgets / limits

The fact is a fixed point over at most a few hundred rows per target, linear per pass; no cap is needed. A closing proof costs one gate run, the same as any proof; the climb costs one post-merge cycle per level.

7. Security & privacy notes

The composer runs inside the step-3 sandbox with no secret, as the outline job does. Submitting needs a credential that can push a branch and open a pull request; Q1 proposes the existing deploy key and merge token rather than a new one. A composed proof is checked by the gate like any proof: no step trusts the fact, so a wrong fact costs a red pull request and never a wrong status.

8. Dependencies

F27 (routes), F07 (post-merge job, merge actor, hole extractor), F08 (uses), D-12 v3.19 and v3.23, D-30.

9. Open questions / judgment calls

IDCall made — for review
F28-Q1Which credential submits: the merge token alone (owner's approval, 2026-10-15, "ok approval, merge, repin, deploy"). Proposed as the deploy key for the push and the merge token for the pull request; built with the merge token for both, since it already holds contents and pull-request write, so one credential gains one reader instead of two gaining a duty each, and the deploy key is untouched. C8 records the new reader. The token cannot start workflows, so the closer does not dispatch the merge actor: each closing pull request's gate run wakes it, as every gate run does.
F28-Q9The precheck a closing proof carries. The bounce rule (D-4) requires an attached passing precheck attestation, and only the service signs one of kind service. A bounce exemption for a file the gate composes itself was built and taken out again the same hour: every target's gate-spec.json already accepts kind none, the unsigned attestation any plain-git contributor may attach, so the closer runs the precheck job's own code in the sandbox and attaches what it writes. No change to D-4, and a closing proof that fails its precheck is never opened (R10).
F28-Q2Derived at read, no schema. Like F27-Q1, the fact is computed from graph.json by one function the MCP and the site share, so it goes live without a re-pin. A product field can follow if a consumer needs it in the file.
F28-Q3A blocked hole with a proof (A23). Whether the record can hold one is not known yet; T1 pins whatever graph.json says, since the fact reads only proved.
F28-Q4Reductions. D-12's artifact 4 has no mode of its own; a reduction arrives as a one-hole partial, so routes already cover it.
F28-Q5A claim's ancestor and the nodes between. circular lists a node's own claims and, under v3.22, the claims that reach nodes on the path between. Only a node's own claims (path prefixed by its id) are upward links; the in-between ones are already covered by routes.
F28-Q6Ruling 1, as built: Lean decides, only the hole's value is rewritten. Printing a whole proof term back to source does not round-trip on Mathlib proofs, so the composer keeps the assembly's text and replaces each hole's value. Lean decides every argument: the hole's goal is unified with each child's conclusion (children tried in the route's order, so holes are matched by statement, never by name or position), each binder the conclusion leaves open is filled from the locals in scope, the one carrying the binder's name first, with backtracking, and the replacement is elaborated against the goal before it is used. Any filling that type-checks is a proof, so a wrong choice can only fail. A local that source cannot name (inaccessible or shadowed) is named by a rename_i at the start of the replacement; on Lean 4.33.1 it reaches shadowed locals too (probed 2026-10-15). A premise a tactic cleared (obtain, rcases, induction) is still a binder of the extractor's child, because the extractor reads the elaborated term; the composer rebuilds it from a constructor whose fields are in scope (⟨k, hk⟩, Or.inl h), any proof of a proposition being as good as another.
F28-Q7A proved have the child needs as a let (case C9). The extractor writes a proved have that a hole's type uses as a let in the child's statement, value and all; the tactic context forgot that value, so the child's conclusion is not the hole's goal there. The composer reads such a let as the same-named local and writes that one have as let, which the whole-file check then confirms. It is the one edit outside a hole's value, and only ever turns a have into a let.
F28-Q8A proof with uses and helpers was refused (T3's finding). A closing proof through a claim or a variant needs a use line (the input's merged proof) and a helper block (the exhibit or relation, made private). Step 2 refused the pair as proof-not-statement: layout.split_uses took use lines out only when the rest began with the whole statement prefix, which a helper block interrupts. Each had been tested alone, and a contributor's proof that uses another node and declares a helper met the same refusal. Fixed in the one reading of a use, so step 2 and staging agree; the helper rules are unchanged and still apply beside a use. A gate change, so live only from the re-pin (D-35).
F28-Q10A hole written := by sorry had no record (the first live run, 2026-10-15). After the re-pin to fa0a13b the closer found exactly erdos-1094--h2--h2--h2--h2 ready and the composer answered "no elaboration record of the hole": for a by value the elaborator records no term info with the block's exact range, only tactic info for the sorry inside it. Twenty-five of the lean tier's cases wrote := sorry; none wrote the live shape. Fixed red first (C20, C20b): such a hole's goal and local context are read from the sorry tactic's record. Nothing was opened: the failure stopped before any pull request, as R10 says.
F28-Q11Proved by construction, not by a pull request per level (the owner's ruling, 2026-10-15, decisions v3.42). "By construction you should be able to close parent nodes. It makes an AND/OR graph. We know that structure. Why do we have to go through this whole thing, one node at a time?" The answer was that v3.41, written at Claude's suggestion, allowed a status only from a merged Proof.lean, which forced each join to be printed as source and sent through a pull request, one level per merge; printing source was where every bug of that day lived (Q6, Q7, Q10, and the live ∀-hole and rewritten-premise failures on erdos-1094). Built instead (T7–T9): the join at the level of terms, kernel-checked, written as the node's compiled module; a closure.json record signed in the post-merge job's one key step; the status derived from it while every input is proved; the whole chain in one run. The owner's two follow-up questions, answered from the code: step 9 is already waived for a root with a certificate, catalog evidence or a completed review (modes.root_vouched applies exactly those rules); and no closure can prove less than the node's own statement, because every link is kernel-checked — the one real exposure, a revised hole weaker than its gap, makes the join fail rather than succeed, which is why the kernel check is required and not optional. The closer workflow, its pull requests, the source composer and the ledger rule were removed.
F28-Q12The circularity claim is no link (the owner's ruling, 2026-10-15, taken in the F08-T42 session; carried here before v3.42 shipped). F28-T1 took a proved hole's own circularity claim as a link to the ancestor it named, and where the hole's siblings were open that link carried real mathematics. Asked whether to keep it, the owner chose one rule: the claim, its label and the link all go, and a proved hole closes what it was cut from only through a route, its siblings proved beside it. This branch carries the link half: closing.links yields routes and variants only, close.LINK_DECLS names the variant's relation only, and closure/v1 and graph/v10 list the kinds route and variant. Red first: test_a_circularity_claim_is_no_link (from F08-T42's branch) failed on this branch's closing.py with the claim link. The claim and its label are retired by F08-T42, which lands after this as decisions v3.43 with the next graph schema.
