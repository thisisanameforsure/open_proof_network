F11 — On-ramp graph intake

Curated intake (D-6): the target layout, the five curator artifacts, fidelity certificates (D-9), registry interop (D-10), the first Mathlib-pinned graph — a classic textbook theorem stated over the graph's own definitions, on the second-class formalization track — and a handful of genuinely open targets listed but not claimable. Ends with the Stage 0 invited-run readiness rehearsal.

1. Summary

Everything before F11 runs on a Mathlib-free tutorial graph. F11 adds what a real target needs: a targets/<id>/ record carrying D-6's five artifacts, a fidelity ladder on the root and definitions, the D-10 posting that makes a target claimable, and a Mathlib pin per graph with the image and caches to match (D-7). It seeds the on-ramp formalization graph the Stage 0 invited run needs — a classic textbook theorem whose objects are defined in the graph's own defs/ so Mathlib cannot close it in one line, decomposed by skeletonization into three to six nodes so the whole pipeline runs to a merged root (Stages, v3.11) — and lists a few open targets imported from Formal Conjectures with grade mechanical-only, so the site is honest about the mission without letting anyone claim them. Cites: D-2, D-6, D-7, D-9, D-10, D-12, D-21, D-25, D-31, D-33, D-35, Stages.

2. User stories

  • As the curator, I want intake to refuse a target missing any of the five artifacts, so nothing listed is under-documented (D-6).
  • As a reviewer, I want the root's fidelity grade and its evidence on the record, and claims blocked until it is at least screened-and-signed (D-6, D-9).
  • As an invited operator, I want a real Mathlib-pinned graph where my agent can close a lemma tonight and see the root close by the end of the run.
  • As a bystander, I want to see the open problems the network exists for, marked as not yet claimable and why.

3. Scope

In: target/v1 and fidelity/v1 schemas; opn-gate intake with the five-artifact check, exclusion-domain check, and scaffolding; fidelity commands; claimability derivation into F03's products; D-10 posting record; Mathlib pinning per graph with per-graph image digest, Mathlib cache in CI, precheck, devcontainer; selection and seeding of the on-ramp result; import of listed open targets from Formal Conjectures at a pinned commit, licence-gated and attributed; the target-status/v2 and targets-index/v2 bump that D-9 v3.12's rung rename forces; a written QA pass per imported statement as evidence; the invited-run readiness rehearsal through both paths and the site.

Non-goals:

  • Tooling for D-9 v3.12's mechanizable pass — the soundness screens, the grounded judge and its review brief, bidirectional equivalence, back-translation — and the upstream drift watcher: all F12. F11 records the grades a curator assigns and, for the five listed targets, the pass run by hand (R13).
  • Upstreaming network-originated statements to Formal Conjectures (D-10) — a curator act documented, not tooled.
  • Any genuinely open target being claimable at Stage 0 (Stages: nothing counts toward open-problem claims).
  • Bounties, dormancy automation, canaries.

F11-R5The system shall provide opn-gate intake post <target> --venue <name> --url <url> recording the D-10 posting and opn-gate intake activate <target> flipping status to active, each a curator PR; activation shall refuse while claimable would remain false, naming the missing condition.
F11-R6When a graph pins a Mathlib SHA, gate-spec.json shall name a per-graph image digest whose image contains that Mathlib's oleans; gate.yml, the precheck job and reproduce.sh shall use it; CI shall run lake exe cache get only on an image miss, and F10's graph cache shall layer on top.
F11-R7The on-ramp target shall be a classic textbook theorem selected against recorded criteria: elementary in an included domain; its objects defined in the graph's own defs/ rather than imported, so no one-line Mathlib citation closes the root; statable in under 40 lines with at most three definitions; decomposable into three to six lemmas each provable by a competent agent in under an hour; the graph still pins Mathlib (D-7) so provers may use its tactics and lemmas beneath the local definitions. The selection record shall name the theorem, the definitions, the planned lemma structure, and two rejected alternatives and why.
F11-R8The on-ramp graph shall be seeded as a root plus a skeleton submitted through F07 (a partial proof citing an annex holding the paper's argument), so its interior nodes are origin: skeleton-hole holes admitted by F08 (amended 2026-09-10, F07-Q13) — the intake path dogfoods the contribution path.
F11-R9When opn-gate intake import-fc <path> --at <commit> runs, the system shall copy the Formal Conjectures statement file at that commit into a new target on the open track with provenance {statement_source: formal-conjectures, path, commit, author} and a sources entry carrying the upstream licence and attribution, grade mechanical-only, status listed, claimable: false, run admission on the root, and open a curator PR; the count at Stage 0 is a config value (default 5). The import shall refuse a source whose licence is outside the allowlist {Apache-2.0, MIT, CC-BY-4.0, BSD-3-Clause} or whose repository is on the denylist (unlicensed auto-merged AI corpora — lean-genius by name, a contamination hazard for the prior-art pass), shall preserve the upstream copyright header of the copied file verbatim, and shall write the attribution into the graph's third-party notice file.
F11-R10The site's Targets page shall show, for a listed open target, the reason it is not claimable (grade, posting, status), the source link, the upstream attribution and licence, and the target's QA summary, so the mission is visible without a claim path. Where a source states no licence (erdosproblems.com), the page shall render the requested citation and a link and shall not reproduce the informal statement; the network's own one-line paraphrase stands in its place.
F11-R12Because D-9 v3.12 renamed the second rung, the system shall publish target-status/v2 and targets-index/v2 carrying the new grade enum, the signature count and the named signers, leaving v1 untouched (D-34: schemas are versioned, never edited); PROTOCOL_VERSION shall move to 3.12 and the F03 product goldens shall be regenerated in the same commit.
F11-R13Before any open target is listed, the curator shall record a written QA pass per imported statement — the D-9 v3.12 pass run by hand, its exhibits, the back-translation comparison, the licence check and the counted external attempts with the statement hash each ran against — as evidence in this repo; F11 tools this no further, and F12 automates it.
F11-R11The invited-run readiness rehearsal shall exercise, with a test identity on each path (git and HTTP/MCP): claim → precheck → submit on an on-ramp node, a postmortem, a variant proposal, the root closing when its last hole merges, the site reflecting each step within one merge; every step's evidence captured with timings.

6. Budgets / limits

  • On-ramp graph gate run under 10 min on a hosted runner with the image hit; precheck under 10 min; image under 15 GiB.
  • Listed open targets at Stage 0: 5.

7. Security & privacy notes

  • Curator identity is recorded on every intake artifact; the curator may not claim proof credit on the target (D-21) — the ledger writer refuses proof entries for the curator on their own target.
  • Imported statements carry upstream authorship in provenance; nothing is re-formalized privately (D-10).
  • Prior-art summaries and evidence text are free text: capped, untrusted downstream.

8. Dependencies

Protocol: this feature is written against decisions v3.12. The rung rename in D-9 and the pin-watching rule in D-10 landed on 2026-09-10; F11 implements the rename, and F12 implements the watcher.

  • F10 (required): image publishing and cache. F08: admission, curator mode. F07: skeleton submission. F04: Targets page.
  • Packages: none new.
  • Carries two earlier criteria. F03-AC13 (products regenerated by a real merge) and F05-AC19 (a claim round trip against the deployed service) cannot be met while the graph holds one proved node and nothing claimable. Seeding the first target closes both: the seeding pull requests exercise the post-merge job, and the open nodes give the frontier something to claim. Decided 2026-09-09 in preference to adding a node to the record purely to satisfy a test (F05-Q6).
  • External: a pinned Formal Conjectures commit; arXiv for the selection; the two D-10 integration agreements are Stage 0 external dependencies and are not blocked on here.
