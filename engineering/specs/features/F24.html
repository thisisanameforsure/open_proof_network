F24 — The steward panel

A problem's stewards decide together, by votes in which only those who vote count. They bring in experts after a proof appears. They publish together, and the problem's page shows its most official write-up.

1. Summary

On 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08 the owner ruled that a complex proof is understood and written up by several people. Those people must work and publish together, and experts may be brought in after a proof appears. A significant share of the sitting stewards must agree before the panel acts. Decisions v3.34 (D-32) is the protocol. F24 builds it:

  • Settings in one place. Every number the panel runs on lives in policy.json (policy/v3). Each value carries its day, its reason and a description. The site shows them on a rules page.
  • Motions and votes. Two new signed records, motion/v1 and vote/v1, are tallied by derivation and never rewritten.
  • Admission. A target's first steward is admitted under the existing switch. Every later steward comes by a passed invitation or by a curator. A cap limits how many problems one person stewards, and a steward who goes quiet lapses.
  • Write-ups. The writeup/v2 record carries a write-up's life from drafted to accepted. The products name the official write-up and its coauthors.

2. User stories

  • As a steward of a resolved problem, I invite an analyst for the analytic step. My co-stewards vote yes over the next two weeks. The analyst accepts and is on the panel.
  • As one of five stewards, I vote on the write-up. Two of us vote and three stay silent, and our two votes decide it.
  • As a steward who helped prove the result, I sign the paper as an author. My vote on its verification is shown but not counted.
  • As a reader, I see the problem's paper at "submitted to Annals", with its arXiv link, and I can open the earlier drafts.
  • As the owner, I change the voting window from 14 days to 21 by one pull request with a reason. The rules page shows the new value and why.

3. Scope

In: the five records and targets-index/v9; the gate's admission of each; the products' tallies, lapse, stages and coauthors; three service routes (POST /motions, POST /votes, POST /writeups) and the invitation path on POST /stewards; the site's panel, write-up and rules views and the /me/ list of what waits.

Out: MCP tools (only a person holds these roles, as in F23); email; chat or discussion (the owner deferred Zulip on 2026-10-08); section claims (declined 2026-10-08); checking a journal's decision against the journal.

4. Requirements

IDRequirement (EARS)
F24-R1policy.json at policy/v3 shall carry panel: vote_threshold, vote_window_days, vote_minimum, vote_minimum_from, steward_cap and steward_lapse_days, each {value, since, reason}. When the file is absent or older, each setting shall take its default (1/2, 14, 2, 3, 5, 183) with null since and reason. A change shall apply only to motions opened after the commit that made it.
F24-R2A motion (targets/<id>/motions/<n>.yaml) shall be opened only by a member of the target's panel or a listed curator. Its window closes vote_window_days after its date. It shall be passed at once when it is an invitation opened by a curator, or when its opener is the only counted member. Otherwise it shall be open until the window closes, then passed when the counted yes votes are at least the threshold share of counted votes, more than the no votes, and at least vote_minimum when the panel had vote_minimum_from or more members at opening. Otherwise it shall be failed.
F24-R3A vote shall count only when it is cast by a member of the panel as it stood on the motion's date, within the window. Only the voter's latest vote counts. On verify-writeup and authorship-threshold, a member who holds proof credit on the target (ledger.holds_proof_line) shall be listed as uncounted. When no member remains counted, listed curators' votes shall count in the panel's place.
F24-R4The panel shall be the target's active stewards who have not lapsed. A steward lapses when their latest signed act on the target is more than steward_lapse_days before the rendered commit's day. Signed acts are their steward records, motions, votes, write-up records and words signatures on the target. A commitment shall be admitted as follows:
  • self only while the target has no panel member, under open admission;
  • a listed curator's login at any time;
  • motion:<n> only when motion n is a passed invitation naming the login.
The gate shall refuse any commitment that takes its login over steward_cap active, unlapsed stewardships. Lapsed stewards shall not count for claimability.
F24-R5A write-up (writeup/v2) shall start with a record naming its authors, made by anyone signed in. Each later act shall be signed by a listed author and name the record. The stage shall be found by climbing the ladder in order, each rung needing the one below it, so that an unreviewed note never outranks a verified paper:
  • drafted: the record names a model;
  • written: the record was made by a person;
  • steward-signed: the record's signer or a signing author is a steward of the target or a curator;
  • panel-verified: a verify-writeup motion naming it passed;
  • released: every author has signed;
  • on-arxiv, submitted, accepted: once released, the highest whose record exists.
A rejected after the latest submitted shall return the stage to on-arxiv, or to released when it has none. A withdrawn shall be final. A writeup/v1 record shall read as a steward-signed record whose only author is its signer, because v1 admitted only stewards and curators. A target is written-up when a paper stands at steward-signed or above.
F24-R6targets/index.json at targets-index/v9 shall publish the panel settings in force and each steward's last_act and lapsed. It shall publish each target's panel: members, and every motion with its state, tallies and uncounted voters.
F24-R7The index shall publish each target's writeups: every write-up with its stage, authors, signers, coauthors and venue, and official. The official write-up is the one at the highest stage, the newest within a stage, never a withdrawn one. Coauthors are panel members who signed the write-up or signed a words section on the target.
F24-R8The service shall accept POST /motions, POST /votes and POST /writeups from a web session, as POST /stewards does. Each shall write the record from the signed-in login, sign it with the approval key, and open an append/ pull request. Before opening anything, it shall refuse by name what the gate would refuse: not a member, window closed, unknown motion, not an author, already signed. POST /stewards shall take a motion number for an invited commitment and shall refuse self when the target has a panel. GET /session shall list the motions awaiting the login's vote and the invitations awaiting their acceptance.
F24-R9The site shall show, on a target page:
  • the panel, with lapsed stewards apart;
  • open and decided motions with their tallies;
  • vote, invite and accept controls for those entitled;
  • the official write-up with its stage on the ladder, and links to the others.
A rules page shall list every panel setting with its value, description, day and reason. /me/ shall list what waits for the reader.
F24-R10The gate shall refuse, by name, the following:
  • a motion, vote or write-up record dated more than one day from its own clock;
  • a vote outside its window, by a non-member, or on a motion that does not exist;
  • a second open invitation for the same login;
  • a write-up act by someone who is not an author, or a second signature by the same author.

6. Budgets / limits

The new routes have the same rate limits as POST /stewards. Records are under 4 KiB. Tallying is linear in the target's records.

7. Security & privacy notes

  • Dates are bound to the gate's clock. The gate refuses a record dated more than a day from its own day, so no one can write a vote into a closed window (R10). This follows the 2026-09-19 rule: when a rule needs "newer", ask who wrote the clock.
  • Every record is signed. The service signs with the approval key, or the person signs with their own SSH key, and the gate verifies against the parent tree's keys/approval.pub, as F23 does.
  • Free text is escaped. A note or a journal name is escaped wherever it is shown. URLs pass the site's allowlist built from validated records (F11-Q11). arXiv identifiers and DOIs become links only through fixed templates.

9. Open questions / judgment calls

IDQuestion / call
F24-Q1A curator adds a steward through an invitation that passes at once. The commitment sentence is the steward's own. A curator therefore cannot write a record for someone else, and the invitee still accepts. A curator's own login in admitted_by also stays valid, which is the reviewed path. Ruled 2026-10-08: "a curator can add a steward without any stall".
F24-Q2The threshold is a fraction. A motion passes when yes votes are at least that share of counted votes and more than the no votes. This makes 1/2 a strict majority and 2/3 an inclusive two-thirds, the owner's "change to 2/3 later" without a second rule. A float would make 2 of 3 fail at 0.666.
F24-Q3Lapse is measured in days. The setting is steward_lapse_days = 183, for the owner's six months, so no calendar arithmetic enters the gate.
F24-Q4"Withdrawn" is a stage of its own. The owner did not answer the question, so this follows the recommendation given on 2026-10-08 and can be reversed in one commit.
F24-Q5A lapsed steward does not count for claimability. A stewarded problem with no act for six months becomes stewardless, which matters only while steward_rule.enforced is true. It is the literal reading of "drops off the panel" and is flagged for the owner.
F24-Q7The ladder is climbed in order. The first draft took the highest stage whose condition held. Its own tests showed a contributor's solo note reading as "released", above a panel-verified paper, because a sole author who signs meets the release condition. Each rung now needs the one below. The arXiv and journal stages need "released", and a contributor's arXiv posting is still linked while the stage stays where it is.
F24-Q8A motion's panel leaves out what the motion itself admits. The service agent found that an invitation passing at once read as open again when the invitee accepted the same day, because their commitment joined the panel of that day. The panel a motion is put to leaves out commitments admitted by that motion or a later one (members(before_motion=n)).
F24-Q9A lapsed steward does not rejoin through their own motion or vote. The gate agent read AC4's "comes back on a new act" as letting a lapsed steward open a motion, and its test pinned that. Decided the other way at merge: a motion is put to the panel as it stood before it, so its own opener's act and votes on it are left out. The usual route back is a new steward record, and the test was restated rather than deleted.
F24-Q10A curator's login is a valid admission under open as well as reviewed. This follows R4 literally ("a listed curator's login at any time"), which is the owner's ruling that a curator may add a steward without a stall. F23's open-admission test was restated to the new rule.
F24-Q11Where each gate rule reads its facts. "Already a member" is judged on the motion's date. "Another open invitation" is judged on the gate's day. The window and eligibility use the motion's own settings. The opener, cap and settings rules read the parent tree's policy.json and curators.json. The gate's day comes from one seam, opn_gate.clock.
F24-Q12The products read the panel on the render day. The render day is the later of the rendered commit's committer day and the target's latest signed act, and never the wall clock (D-5). A record dated ahead of its commit, such as one signed in a timezone ahead of UTC, would otherwise drop its own signer off the panel. has_paper reads the motions as of the first possible day, which cannot change its answer, because its floor sits below every rung a motion decides.
F24-Q13A self-commitment is judged without the committer. A sole steward committing again as self passes, because the rule asks whether anyone else sits on the panel.
F24-Q14Site gaps to look at.
  • /rules/ reads the admission switch from policy.json, because v9 does not publish it.
  • A member sees Yes and No after voting and can change the vote.
  • Withdrawing carries no reason, because writeup/v2 has no field for one.
  • There is no control to open an authorship-threshold motion; the service route accepts one.
F24-Q6Where the drafted and written stages come from. They are reached only through write-up records, which anyone signed in may now make. They are not reached through a proof's explainer, which F20 and F21 already label. The index's stage is about papers and notes.
