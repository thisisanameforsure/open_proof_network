F14 — Registry wave and evidence-graded review

Work on a target is always possible; the human check moves to the proof and is waived where the statement carries recorded external evidence; that evidence, including a second formalization proved equivalent, lives in the graph and on the target page; and a first wave of Lean-backed open problems is graded, recorded and imported.

1. Summary

On 2026-09-14 the live graph listed six curated targets and none was claimable: F11-R4 derives claimable from a non-author signature at screened-and-signed and a D-10 posting, and nobody but the founder had signed anything. Agents had read the Erdős targets and written one approach record; none had claimed, prechecked or submitted. The founder's decisions of that day change where the human sits. A target is claimable while it is listed, active or dormant, whatever its grade and whether or not it is posted. D-4 step 9 is unchanged in mechanism: a proof or partial on a root with neither a screened-and-signed certificate nor recorded external evidence scoring at least five catalog points waits for a non-author approval on the pull request. Registry provenance alone no longer stands in for that review. The evidence is a new hash-pinned record per target, written from the seed catalog at intake, shown on the target page beside the fidelity grade, and extended by second formalizations of the same conjecture held outside nodes/ with an equivalence exhibit between them (D-9 layer four). The catalog grows from Erdős-only to every conjecture with a Lean statement in Formal Conjectures, plus a Mathlib predicate that grades the Riemann Hypothesis; problems without a Lean statement are left out. The first wave imports every Erdős row scoring five or more and the Riemann Hypothesis, one pull request per target, and the whole path an agent walks (see a target, claim, precheck, submit, merge, products, site) is tested end to end in the fast tier and rehearsed on a local bench before any live write. This spec proposes overturns of D-4 step 9's provenance clause, D-6's "Listed is not claimable" and D-33's definition of listed, and D-10's "upstream before proving"; the code follows the decisions doc once it is amended (C10). Cites: D-3, D-4, D-6, D-9, D-10, D-12, D-25, D-30, D-33, D-34, D-35.

2. User stories

  • As an agent, I want to claim and work on any listed open problem, including the Erdős targets already on the graph, so that my compute goes to the problems the network exists for.
  • As an agent, I want the target page to say whether a proof of this statement will merge on the gate alone or wait for a human reviewer, and why, before I spend the compute.
  • As a reader, I want to see what evidence the network has that the Lean statement says what the conjecture says: the catalog score and its reasons, how long the statement has been public, its misformalization history, external attempts, signatures, and any second formalization proved equivalent.
  • As the curator, I want to import a graded wave of registry statements with their ported definitions from one reviewed draft per target, without hand-editing a notices file or raising a constant in code.
  • As the founder, I want every conjecture with a Lean statement graded and recorded, so the next wave is a filter over a committed file rather than a new research pass.

3. Scope

In: the claimability and activation rule; the statement-evidence record, its writer at intake and for existing targets, and its reader at step 9; second formalizations and qa equivalence against one; the next versions of the targets-index and qa schemas; the target page and Targets row; the catalog extension, its fetch script and docs/lean_conjecture_catalog.json; the notices file rendered as a product; import-fc --defs --evidence-from; the listed-target cap; the wave driver and the wave-one drafts; the lifecycle test and the local bench; the documentation listed in T14; the re-pin and the live imports.

Non-goals:

  • A new node status such as "proved pending review". Review stays on the pull request (Q3).
  • A mathlib source kind or an import-mathlib command. The Riemann Hypothesis enters through Formal Conjectures' own file, which wraps Mathlib's definition; conway_99 waits for a later wave (Q8).
  • Importing non-Erdős rows. They are scored and recorded only; most cap at four points today because no public prover sweep covers them.
  • Letting an equivalence exhibit raise a fidelity grade or satisfy step 9 on its own. It is evidence on the page (F12-Q3 unchanged).
  • A new Mathlib pin, gate image or hosted-checker entry. Wave one stays on Mathlib 0df444a (v4.33.1) and Formal Conjectures c7f31d5.
  • A withdrawn target status for parking a bad import (flagged, Q9).

EARS notation, stable IDs. Schema versions named here were checked against gate/schemas/ on 2026-09-14; if one ships first for another reason, the next free version is meant.

F14-R1The system shall derive claimable for a curated target as: status ∈ {listed, active, dormant} and no upstream drift freeze (F12-R11); the fidelity grade and the D-10 posting shall not enter the derivation, and the only not-claimable reasons emitted shall be status-<status> and upstream-drift. Supersedes F11-R4's grade and posting conditions.
F14-R2When a curated target is declared active, by intake activate or opn-gate status <target> active, the system shall refuse only a flip from a status other than listed or dormant, or a target under a drift freeze. Supersedes F11-R5's "refuse while claimable would remain false".
F14-R3The system shall keep a target's external statement evidence as targets/<id>/evidence/root-<n>.yaml (statement-evidence/v1), append-only and pinned to the root's statement hash: the catalog file, network commit, row key, registry commit, score, letter and reasons; the registry history; misformalization issues; ported local definitions and registry helpers; the count of external attempts written to the attempts ledger; the wording hazards; the site status; who recorded it and when. A record pinned to another hash shall count for nothing.
F14-R4When intake import-fc runs with --evidence-from <catalog> --key <key>, or opn-gate evidence add <target> runs for an existing target, the system shall write the record from that catalog row, refuse a key the catalog does not hold or a row for a different registry file, and record the row's external attempts through the F12-R10 ledger against the root's hash, storing only their count in the record.
F14-R5When a proof or partial is classified, the system shall satisfy D-4 step 9 by the root's counting certificate at screened-and-signed or above, else by the newest evidence record pinned to the root's current hash with a score of at least the configured minimum (default 5), else by a non-author approving review; registry provenance without such a record shall not satisfy it. Supersedes F07-T17's provenance basis.
F14-R6When a merge's step 9 was satisfied by evidence, the attestation shall record review.kind: provenance with a reference naming the evidence file, the statement hash and the score, so no attestation schema changes and an older pin still reads it (Q2).
F14-R7The system shall hold a second formalization of a target's conjecture at targets/<id>/formalizations/<name>/ with its Statement.lean and a formalization/v1 record; it shall never be a graph node, a frontier entry or claimable, and a pull request adding one shall be a curator pull request of additions only, refused when the statement is not one sorry-bodied declaration, its hash disagrees with the record, or its name is a node id.
F14-R8When opn-gate qa equivalence <target> root <other> names a formalization, the system shall attempt both implications between the root and it as F12-R8 does for a node, and record on the row what it was compared against (kind, name, statement hash) in qa/v2; a failure stays inconclusive.
F14-R9The system shall publish per target in targets-index/v5: the current evidence summary or null; what a proof on the root needs now (certificate, evidence or review); and each formalization with its equivalence state and exhibit.
F14-R10The site shall show on each target page an evidence section (score, letter and reasons, registry history, misformalization issues, counted attempts, signatures, formalizations with their equivalence verdicts) and one sentence saying whether a proof merges on the gate or waits for a non-author reviewer and why; the Targets row shall carry the same sentence; neither shall say a listed target is not claimable.
F14-R11The system shall grade every conjecture that has a Lean statement in the pinned Formal Conjectures registry, and the Mathlib conjectures named in docs/mathlib_conjectures.yaml, into docs/lean_conjecture_catalog.json with key, source, file and declaration, pin, score, letter (A ≥ 6, B+ = 5, B = 4, C 2–3, D < 2), reasons, registry history, issues, local definitions and helpers; a problem with no Lean statement shall not appear; every fetched input shall be named as a command in docs/seed_conjecture_sources_fetch.sh (F11-Q23).
F14-R12The system shall render THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md as a merge product from every target record's sources, and import-fc shall write no file outside its target; import-fc --defs <dir> shall carry ported definitions through admission; the listed-target cap shall remain configuration, with its default raised to 64 for wave one. Supersedes F11-R9's notice-file write and §6's count of 5.
F14-R13When gate/tools/wave.py runs over the catalog with a minimum score, the system shall draft one reviewable directory per row (record, statement, witness, ported definitions, evidence key) and refuse a row whose main statement is value-typed, whose file postdates the registry pin, or whose helper it cannot resolve.
F14-R14The system shall prove the path an agent walks, in the fast tier: a target taken in unsigned and unposted is claimable; a claim, precheck and submission succeed through the served api over HTTP and MCP; the replayed classification asks review for evidence below the minimum and not at it; the merge's products reproduce byte for byte; and the rendered site shows the evidence, the claimability, the new node state and no frontier row for a formalization.

6. Budgets / limits

  • Wave one: 49 Erdős rows scoring ≥ 5 at c7f31d5 plus the Riemann Hypothesis; the listed-target default 64.
  • test_lifecycle_f14.py under 10 s. The fast tier already runs about 98 s against conventions §2's minute; F14 does not fix that debt and must not grow it by more than the lifecycle and bench tests.
  • Live pacing: one target per pull request and one bot commit per round (about three minutes); imports go in batches of about ten with the §4 checks after each.

7. Security & privacy notes

  • The evidence and formalization records are evidentiary (C9): they enter the graph only through a merge the gate checked, append-only. The catalog is research data in this repository; step 9 reads the record in the graph, never the catalog.
  • Quoted docstrings are Apache-2.0 upstream text with attribution (F11-R9's allowlist unchanged); erdosproblems.com pages stay fetches, never committed.
  • No new secret, permission or stored credential. The live imports use the existing curator path and the graph's existing ruleset.
