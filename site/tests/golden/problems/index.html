Legend: what the terms mean ▾
open
No proof has closed this statement and nothing blocks starting on it. Anyone may work on it now.
needs a witness
Left open by a proof skeleton and waiting only for its witness. Supplying one is the work here and anyone may do it; once it merges, the statement is open for proof.
blocked
Waits on other statements, or on a definition that has not been checked yet. Not accepting work. A statement waiting only for its witness is not blocked in this sense: it needs a witness.
proved
A proof passed all nine checks and was merged. Proved is not the same as explained.
Explained
A person who can explain the proof without the tool that produced it has signed the explainer. This is the count the network is judged by.
Written up
A paper or note about the proof exists.
Steward
The named mathematician who has committed to understand and write up whatever the network produces on a problem. A problem without one does not accept work.
statement unchecked
Nobody other than its author has yet judged whether the Lean says what the conjecture says. The kernel cannot decide this; a person must.
Work on
A courtesy signal to others that you are working on a statement. It expires on its own and never blocks anyone.
