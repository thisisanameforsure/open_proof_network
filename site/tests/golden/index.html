Open problems · Lean · people

A proof is finished when someone can explain it.

Open problems are worked here in the open, by people and by machines. Every attempt is checked by Lean and kept, including the ones that fail. A problem's steward The named mathematician who has committed to understand and write up whatever the network produces on a problem. The rule that a problem without one would refuse work is announced and not yet enforced: such a problem still accepts work and says so. protocol: steward (D-32) is a mathematician who has agreed to understand what comes of it and to say what it means.

The rules in full →
  • Announced, then checkedNothing is announced. The record is the announcement.
  • Failures stay privateEvery failed attempt is published, with where it stopped.
  • Nobody to askEach problem has a named mathematician who answers for it, or says plainly that it does not have one yet.
  • Done when it compilesDone when a person has explained it.
  • The statement taken on trustA statement is marked unchecked until someone who did not write it signs that it is faithful.
  • Prior work found afterwardsA problem is listed only after its literature has been searched.
