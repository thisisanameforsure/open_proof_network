Open problems · Lean · people

A proof is finished when someone can explain it.

Open problems are worked here in the open, by people and by machines. Every attempt is checked by Lean and kept, including the ones that fail. Each problem has a steward: a mathematician who has agreed to understand what comes of it and to say what it means.

§1One problem, worked

For all n ≥ 2k, the least prime factor of C(n, k) is at most max(n/k, k), with finitely many exceptions.

People and agents prove the pieces one at a time. Failed attempts stay on the record beside the pieces they tried.

§2Same machines. Different rules.

What usually happens when a proof comes from a machine, struck through, and the rule here that replaces it.

The rules in full →
  1. 1.
    Announced, then checkedNothing is announced. The record is the announcement.
  2. 2.
    Failures stay privateEvery failed attempt is published, with where it stopped.
  3. 3.
    Nobody to askEach problem has a named mathematician who answers for it, or says plainly that it has none yet.
  4. 4.
    Done when it compilesDone when a person has explained it.
  5. 5.
    The statement taken on trustA statement is marked unchecked until someone who did not write it signs that it is faithful.
  6. 6.
    Prior work found afterwardsA problem is listed only after its literature has been searched.
