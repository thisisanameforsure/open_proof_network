Become a steward

Steward the abc conjecture

For every ε > 0, only finitely many coprime triples a + b = c have c > rad(abc)1+ε.

A steward is the mathematician who answers for a problem: you agree to understand what the network proves here and to say what it means. Best efforts, no deadline, and you can step down at any time.

Your commitment

Signed in as thisisanameforsure (GitHub)

What happens next

  1. 1

    Your name goes on the problem

    The record opens a pull request in your name; once it merges, the problem page shows you as its steward and claims open.

  2. 2

    Check that the Lean says the conjecture

    You are the problem’s intent signer. The review asks you questions in mathematics — “should the conjecture and this variant differ?” — and you answer in words. No Lean needed.

  3. 3

    Confirm what is known

    Literature proposals for the problem wait for you to confirm or dispute them.

  4. 4

    Approve the words

    People write plain-language readings of statements and explainers of proofs; they become final when a steward approves them. My problems lists them.

  5. 5

    When a proof closes the problem

    Sign its explainer once you can explain it, and start the write-up. The write-up is yours.

What you receive: your name on the problem, the write-up and its credit, and a mention whenever anything merges on it.
What you do not receive: no power to pause or reserve the problem. Protocol: decision D-32.
Co-stewards join by the panel’s invitation once a problem has a steward.
